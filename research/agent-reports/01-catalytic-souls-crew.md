## 3. Events timeline (CONFIRMED unless noted)

- **Gemini Moon Cave Fest**, Apr 15–16, 2005 (Southern Illinoisan, Apr 7, 2005).
- **UnderGround Sound 1 "Mid West Freak Fest"**, June 18, 2005, Shawnee Cave (forum).
- **Cavestock II "The Harvest"**, Sept 23–24, 2005 — Cornmeal headlined, 10+ bands, $12 adv/$20 gate (Southern, Sept 22, 2005).
- Fall 2005 club tours: Face of Bass (STL/Jackson), Technomeister, PsychoTronic w/ FACTORe (St. Louis, Memphis, Jackson MS), NYE 2005 with Disco Productions in St. Louis.
- **CaveFest 06 "420 edition"**, Apr 21–23, 2006 (Cornmeal).
- **UGS 2 "Back 2 the Freak"**, June 30–July 2, 2006 — 18 acts incl. Infected Mushroom, Electric Skychurch, John B, DJ Monk, Kid Icarus, Elephantronic, Riot Squad, Unkl Ryan (Southern, June 29/July 6, 2006; Shroomery thread May 2006).
- Cave Jam Solstice (June 23–25, 2006, Vince Herman) and Cavestock 3 (Sept 22–23, 2006) — promoted via the CS forum.
- **UGS 3**, July 6–8, 2007 — YouTube "Underground Sound 3 - 7-7-07 - Shawnee Cave Rave" (chickenpotpie43, uploaded 2009-12-27, 779 views). Aftermath: Southern Illinoisan, July 9–10, 2007 (Adam Testa): "at least four arrested during weekend festival"; security firm Five-"O" "contracted by cave owner Bob Goodale and the promoters of the Underground Sound Festival" claimed it burned confiscated drugs/knives from ~15 people.
- **Gamma Fest** (Shpongle DJ set + Hallucinogen), Aug 17–18, 2007, Eureka Music Park, Eureka Springs, AR — CS with "Twisted" (first out-of-state festival).
- **Cavefest 2008**, Apr 18–20, 2008 — "more than 25 acts… hosted by CatalyticSouls" (Southern, Apr 17, 2008); Daily Egyptian, Apr 18, 2008 ("Cave Fest set to rock this weekend": techno tent + cave stage, $20/$30).
- **UGS 4**, Aug 15–17, 2008, Shawnee Cave — RA event 57997 (search snippet): Hallucinogen live, Shpongle DJ set, Jackal & Hyde, Evol Intent, Tommie Sunshine, DJ ESP, Baby Anne. Last CS event at the cave.
- **UGS 5**, Aug 14–15, 2009, **Camp Zoe, Salem MO** (campzoe.com archived): Bassnectar, Astral Projection, Donald Glaude, Tech Itch, Dylan, EOTO, Jackyl & Hyde, Fort Knox Five, Woody McBride, Noise Floor Crew, Trash Yourself!, Keith MacKenzie, VJ Benji Ramsey, The Dervish, Tom Foolery, Andy B, Dr. Spook A/V; $70 adv/$100 gate.
- **UGS 6 "Freakadelic"**, Aug 13–15, 2010, Camp Zoe: Rabbit in the Moon 3D, Angel Alanis, Bro Safari, Charles Feelgood, EOTO, Eskmo, Evol Intent live, Excision, Heyoka, Jackal & Hyde, Lynx & Janover, Menace to Sobriety, Noise Floor Crew, Paul Anthony, Rockin Roland vs John Doe, Tekfro, TomFoolery, Tommy "I.C." Gunns, VibeSquad, Woody McBride, Zilla; visuals Benji Ramsey (flyer + campzoe.com).
- **UGS 7 "The Freaky Tiki"**, July 21–24, 2011, **Hogrock Ranch, Cave-in-Rock IL** (theuntz.com archived; Southern Illinoisan concerts listing July 20, 2011 gives contact CatalyticSouls@gmail.com and catalyticsouls.com/ugs): 5 stages; Shpongle "Shpongletron Experience", Christopher Lawrence, DJ Dan, Bassbin Twins, Liquid Stranger, Eprom, Starkey, Michal Menert, DMC champ Shiftee, Dara, Nigel Richards, Noel Sanger, Lo IQ?, plus crew: TomFoolery, Andy B, Tommy I.C. Gunns, Derve, Reperkushin, Tekfro. Tickets $65–$115 presale, gate up to $140; 18+. UNTZ copy: "Since 2005, tens of thousands of people have been a part of the UnderGround Sound experience."
- **Inference**: UGS 7 was the last edition — no UGS 8 appears in the Jan 2012 site capture, the LLC's active window ended Aug 2011, and the domain lapsed by 2013.

Venue context: Shawnee Cave manager/part-owner Shane Wade told The Daily Yonder (May 26, 2023) that "in the 2000s, it became an underground EDM scene, which… kind of got out of hand… By probably 2008 or 2009 it shut down. My partner acquired it in 2012 or 2013." The venue reopened 2020–21 for bluegrass/jam festivals (Shawnee Cave Revival, Weekend at the Cave). Note a conflict: showcaves.com says it was bought by "Jeff Parrish" in 2004, while 2007 press names owner Bob Goodale.
