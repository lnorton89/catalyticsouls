.::Cornmeal

While keeping in the tradition of musical acts such as Old and in the Way, John Hartford, and New Grass Revival, Cornmeal has continued to push the boundaries of traditional bluegrass music for more than five years. Known for their solid musicianship, rich vocal harmonies, Cornmeal has proven to be a moving presence in the world of Americana roots music.

With the release of their debut album "In The Kitchen" (2001 Livin' Live records), Cornmeal secured its place as one of the top bands in its genre. On their second effort, "Tales from Magic Stone Mountain" (2003 Livin' Live records), the band pushes its style further with a blend of 13 original songs recorded at the famed Rax Trax studio in Chicago. This self-produced album is proof again that Cornmeal has captured the heart of Americana with a thoughtful and inspiring performance.

Cornmeal currently performs more than 160 shows per year throughout the region and beyond, and has gained valuable exposure and attracted a faithful following from Colorado to the Carolinas. While playing a blend of high profile venues, jam-band and bluegrass festivals to thousands throughout the region, the band has shared the stage with such highly acclaimed national acts as THE DAVID GRISMAN QUINTET, JOHN HARTFORD, THE DEL McCOURY BAND, LITTLE FEAT, LEFTOVER SALMON, TONI RICE, YONDER MOUNTAIN STRING BAND, DARK STAR ORCHESTRA, moe, SAM BUSH and UMPHREY'S McGEE.

In addition, Cornmeal has employed a vigorous college, AAA and internet radio campaign, including the addition to the Home Grown Music Radio Program, helping to broaden their reach to listeners from coast to coast.
(Select Radio Station Listing and Press )

Heavily influenced by American roots and folk music, Cornmeal began as a side project over five years ago only to see it become a true Chicago institution. Performing tirelessly, Cornmeal prides itself on the energy it puts forth each night, creating a powerful, unrivaled live performance experience.

http://www.cornmealinthekitchen.com





.::The Station:

The Station is a four-piece progressive jam-rock band out of Springfield, IL. The Station consists of Dave Littrell (vocals, saxes, guitar), Josh Kerska (Bass), Dave Carter (drums/percussion/vocals), and Kevin Lemen (guitar). By blending genres such as funk, bluegrass, rock, jazz and blues,The Station creates irresistible grooves felt throughout the audience. Well-crafted songs, improvisational jams and experimenting with different musical styles are at the heart of what The Station brings to the stage. Throughout the course of a night,The Station is bound to explore all types of music. One song will be all-out funk,driven by sax and bass,while the next will be characterized by group improvisation,where floating guitar lines weave in and out of one another.On the other hand, the next song after that may be more composed and crafted. Whether sitting back and letting The Station sink in, or grooving all night long,everyone involved shares in a unique experience.The Station has shared bills with such national acts as moe.,Umphrey's Mcgee,Karl Denson, The Big Wu,Leftover Salmon,Ekoostik Hookah,Jazz Mandolin Project,Oteil and the Peacemakers,Charlie Hunter,Dark Star Orchestra,and Keller Williams, among other various notable regional acts. The Station was named winner of the annual "Battle of the Bands" on Halloween (10/31/02) at Cicero's in St. Louis. Since then they've made their way through Iowa, Indiana, Illinois, Missouri, Tennessee, Kentucky, Mississippi, Arkansas, Colorado and Kansas, continuing to take the midwest by storm. The bands studio efforts include FUMUNDA (2000), all that lies between...(2004), and "Playground"(2005).

http://myspace.com/thestationmusic

.::Defined Perception:

Defined Perception is Southern Illinois' premier jam band. Based out of Carbondale the five members connect with each other to ignite a frenzy of grooves, comprised of ragin' guitar solos and screaming organs laid over an outstanding array of percussion and solid bass. By mixing touches of rock, bluegrass, funk, techno, jazz, Latin, and reggae they have created a unique style that appeals to many diverse audiences. The name is what you want to take from it. To each their own-- Define your own perception! The drummer, Paul Krajewski, came up with the name about two years ago with a kid he used to jam with. Soon thereafter the two of them meet their bass player, Josh Beal. A few months went by when while working at a local pizza place Josh meet Steven Kaufman, who joined the band and immediately changed the feel of the jams. After a few tryouts, a little drama, and a lot of drinkin', the guys completed their group when Ryan Beavers joined on percussion, and Steven's long time friend and musical partner in crime Jesse Payne moved to C'dale to join the ensemble. The band has played many venues around the C'dale area, including Hanger 9, Booby's, Tres Hombres, PK's, and the awesome Saltpeter Cave, aka Shawnee Amphitheater. Plans for the future include laying the rest of their first album in the studio, practice, practice, practice, mid-west touring, working with their new promoter, and kickin’ it in the woods. The music is motivating; high energy jams that are guaranteed to make your body dance! Check out a few of the songs here on the website and see for yourself. If your local, you may even find DP at some random house party blowin' it up! I've heard the guys say, "We just love to play!!"

http://www.myspace.com/DefinedPerception

.:: DJ Scoto (OPTIXmusic Productions, Toymaker Promotions)
<Hard Techno, Funky Techno, Acid Techno, Hard House, Hardcore, Breaks>

Scott Danesi (aka DJ Scoto) has been producing various genres of electronic music since 1996. He started under the name MATRIX along with Steve Fox. Steve and Scott released their first self titled album in 1998 which sold locally for the next 6 months. Soon after the release of the album there was an unexpected need for them to change the name of their group. Scott then changed the name to OPTIX. This is when OPTIXmusic Productions was created. Scott then began to produce a solo album titled, The Orbit. It was then released in 1999 under OPTIXmusic Productions. Under OPTIXmusic Productions, Scott began to throw house parties in Carbondale in the year 2000 and made appearances as DJ Scoto at other locations. He then began producing again with Katy Rushin and Wade Van Ommering in 2001 under the name OPTIX. They soon released their first album together called FUSION. This album was sold locally in Carbondale, IL for the next year. Scott then started working by himself again in 2003 and started producing harder tracks that he was able to play at the house parties and events that he was spinning at. It was then in 2004 when he got hooked up with Toy Maker Productions out of Champaign, IL.

After a bunch of work and anticipation Scott has released his first vinyl with various hard techno tracks on it. SC7-001 (Catalog number of the vinyl), released March 15th 2005, has 5 tracks including his Teenage Wasteland Remix, Communication Terminated, Wrong Direction, Sex Drive, and a super secret hidden track. You really can't have a hidden track on a vinyl, but he likes it there.

Starting in early 2005, Scott discovered the art of circuit bending. If you don't know what that is, it is when a toy is modified by connecting switches and resisters to the circuit board which cause it to short out and make very abstract noises. He is currently using a circuit bent Speak and Read, some cheap Wal-Mart piano, and the infamous Nintendo Entertainment System. A few months after he created these freakish toys, he released his first track with a cameo from each of them. The track is a remix of "Tom Sawyer" by Rush which is available in the downloads section of his website.

After his Live PA debut at CaveFest 2005, Scott was invited back to perform on the main stage of Midwest Freakfest in June of 2005. He then created his alternate ego, Dr. Destructo. He now uses this name when he does his live PA of circuit bending madness.

To pay respect to the passing of legendary Robert Moog, Scott decided to start tooling around with DSP (Digital Signal Processing) to create his own VST synthesizer. After about a month of design it was finally ready to be tested and used. This VST Instrument is called Old School and is like no other in existence. What makes his synthesizer different is that the 3 main oscillators can detune from 0% to 100% with a turn of one knob. This VST will be available for download on his website as soon as it is stable enough.

In September of 2005, Scott released his first breaks track to the public. This track titled, Angel to Angel Remix, was an entry for a remix contest of Dave Angel and Angel Alanis. This song is available in the downloads section for his website. This song also features Scotts Old School VST Synthesizer.



.:J MD & Jack Herrer (THC Syndicate/ Abandoned House Productions / Masonic Productions / PhD Productions/ Spoololife Technologies)
<Speed Garage>

MD started spinning house music @ local house parties in 1997 and then moved to Bloomington in 1999. He continued to play at several different house parties and after parties and began to throwing small after hours parties in Indianapolis. In 2000he fell in love with garage music, and has been mixing all forms of the genre since. He played his first rave (Ultra Violet Dreams) in January of 2001 @ Drifters in Indianapolis under his original D.J. moniker (Majickly Delicious In Febuary of 2001.

I played at the Blacklight Kama Sutra party in Bloomington, and I also played a couple of gigs at Axis nightclub in Bloomington the same year. During all of this I was also throwing small events in Bloomington at various venues, and after hours parties in Indy primarily at the Festivilla. In May of the same year I met one of the most influential and talented D.J.s I have ever met; Alex PI (owner of 2Recordz)( www.2recordz.com). We played an after hours in Bloomington together entitled "Smell My Skunk". I then moved to Rotterdam Holland for three months to study under Alex and his Dutch friends and hone my mixing skills.

While in Holland, MD played at Club Da Rage on South Gravendijkwal in the section of Rotterdam known as the Niewe Binnenweg. When he returned to the US he relocated to Terre Haute (his home town), and started a 7 month long residency mixing and scratching gangsta rap in the roughest club I've ever worked at the Spin Cycle. After returning from a snowboarding hiatus in Colorado, he started a weekly residency on 90.7 F.M. (WMHD, Terre Haute) playing his usual 2-Step and 2x4 Speed Garage sets with Jack Herrer.

I played "BBQ Heaven" for Slater Hogan in June of 04 at Fusion in Indy. I threw "Spark" in Bloomington on June 25th. I played Fusion for Slater again in July "Muzique Tropique" (7-03-04). I also played Still Clownin for Geek in Bloomington two weeks later (7-17-04). I threw another party in Bloomington called "Ignition" on July 30th. After this, I started playing what was essentially a semi-weekly residency at Fusion with Jack Herrer for the next 7 months. Notable events during this time period are Flashback (September), Pilgrims and Indians (November), Afterbirth (January), and several other "Naptown Underground" events including the Frankie Bones event on February 19th of 2005. During this time period Jack Herer and I were added to the roster of PhD productions (Pharmy's company), and I was added to Spoololife Technologies (Jimmi Journey's company)(www.spoololife.com). I won the Indiana State University scratch battle with a broken right hand in January (beating my homebaoy A.C. de Selectah in the final round). I threw "Jah-Electro" in March of 2005 at the Verve in Terre Haute, and played "Aftermath" at the Mirage in Indy. I played "No Laughing Matter" in early April for Geek's company (Supa Star Amp). I played "Release" at Harmony Shools in Bloomington for Jimmy Journey in April of 2005, and I threw "Where's the Beat?" in Terre Haute on April 30th. I was a last minute addition to the "Midwest Freakfest" in southern Illinois (June 17th-19th). I was added to the roster of a Dallas based production company called the "Abandoned House Crew" in June. I played another city-sponsored event in July of 05 "Live Music Lunch", and threw a small event at the Fourth Quarter in Terre Haute called "Evening Beats". That brings me to today. Everything that I have done is not listed here, due to my excessive weed smoking and subsequent lack of memory, but this is a good summation of my career.

.:: Jimmi Journey (House & Techno)
<Spoololife Technologies / Masonic Productions / THC Syndicate>

A Jack of all trades, Jimmi has managed to build a vast repertoire of artistic mediums by which he expresses a love for EDM culture. With 6 years behind the 1s & 2s Jimmi maintains a focus on reaching further galaxies, teaching EDM Art concepts in K-12 Schools, spearheading benefits for the American Red Cross, and assisting promoters, domestic & international, through networking and a strong relationship with Spoololife Technologies... where he and a team of dedicated EDM advocates promote, DJ, and produce EDM and Culture.
A technologist, graphic designer, concept developer, DJ, and Producer Jimmi manages to stay engrossed in EDM around the clock. Though always with many projects on the "tables", Jimmi is dedicated to his craft as a DJ, mixing sets composed of a variety of genres and styles. With a WHOOT! level of dedication, its no surprise to have seen Jimmi on bills with Paul Johnson, DJ Nitro, DJ Booth, Scott Hardkiss, 3D, Kos & Xavier, 7up, Mark Almaria, Wilhelm K.. ,the Detroit Grand Pubas, Translucent, and Tommy Sunshine to name a few. Regionaly recognized for his creativity and innovation, Jimmi has moved dancefloors throughout the Midwest including but not limited to St. Louis, Cleveland, Kalamazoo, Nashville, and all over his home state of Indiana.

Looking forward, Jimmis latest studio work, the Dont Stop Steppin EP, is scheduled for release on Spoololife Technologies in 2006, celebrating his 6th year as a DJ, and 10th year as a producer.
