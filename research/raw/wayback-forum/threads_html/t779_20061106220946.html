ToyMaker Is Proud To Present Another Mid-West Event......

BarnYard Boogie 5
-Til TheCows Come Home...


Date: November 4th, 2006
Time: 8 PM to 8 AM
Cost: $11 presale before Nov. 2nd, 2006, $15 @ the door or $14 w/Flyer
also please bring $1 donation for the Leukemia and Lymphoma Society!!
Location: Central ILL-Noise

Tickets are available at
Groovetickets or
Trounce Records

Well, here we go, again! Bringing talent from all over the country, this
event is not to be missed! Over 30 DJs will be playing. ToyMaker has secured
a venue near PEORIA, IL with not one, but two sound stages!!! This event
will no doubt surpass the previous four events and set a new standard for
ToyMaker events. It will be a 12 hour event, starting at 8 PM and ending at
8 AM, on November 4th 2006. With state of the art sound systems and lighting
powered by Outlawed Productions, Maximum Strength, ToyMaker, and many more.

Call the info lines at 262-364-3124 or 217-215-4091 for more information, or
visit
www.ToyMakerMusicPromotions.itgo.com.

You can BYOB at this event but, absolutely NO DRUGS, NO MARKERS, NO WEAPONS
And NO BAD VIBES! And as always water will be free, but a limited quantity
will be available. So bring some to share as well. Feel free to campout at
this event, especially if you plan to drink. We would rather you stay than
drive home drunk. So be smart, and do the right thing. At this event P.L.U.R
and R.O.A.R will be in full effect. Please respect the venue and our scene.
Take this opportunity to enjoy what brings us all together -MUSIC- Be safe
and well see you there.

Lineup:

Avex Axiom - Chicago, IL~(Blueline records / zuvuya
records / Puzzld Music / Yin Yang / IYS/MNL)
With his adaptation of EFX processors, a loop factory, and his three deck
sessions mixed in harmony and with perfection; Avex Axiom has been storming
through the United States and abroad, playing high profile events such as
the WMC, Movement, Burning Man, and Caveman. Avex Axiom has also been
recognized on over 50 radio stations including XM radio, and featured on
countless websites. Recently Avex has ventured out to Germany for a few
events as well as prospecting to move out to berlin and moving forward his
music career.

Submerge 101 - Chicago, IL ~(Impact Mechanics /
Psychotik Records)
Chicago techno artist Orlando Solis has built a solid reputation around the
US as an innovative dance floor techno DJ .His Dj apprenticeship began with
industrial music at the age of 15.His multi-styled turntable skills have
developed into a precise compound of dexterity, texture, rhythm and style.
No introduction must be made for the techno consanguine, as he represents
the ultimate in energetic, hard but funky house and European techno. This
will be an exciting year for one of Chicago's leading techno artist as he
releases several tracks with remixes due this year by, Robert Armani,
Hyperactive, Bam Bam and Mark Verbos. He has been heralded as one of the
best up and coming techno acts by many respected and known artists such as
Richie Hawtin, T-1000 and many others.

Identity - Lisbon Falls, ME ~(NERU / One Luv /
ToyMaker Prod. / Underground)
One of America's rising female DJ's hits the streets, again to proclaim the
longevity of dance music. Her newest mix, 'Hard House Domination,' Volume 1
will be hitting the streets with her the rest of the year as she takes dates
across the globe.

Christopher Owens - Cincinnati, OH ~ (ToyMaker Prod.)
Christopher sticks to his guns, he is a true house soldier from beginning to
end. It will always be house!!! Bangin, Driving and Funky... He is very much
a product of the electronic scene. Always extremely active behind the decks,
He loves to add a lot more than meets the eye to his sets, making his style
and his sound his very own.

Mark Almaria - Chicago, IL ~(Funk Tech / IHR /
Bunchlox Music)
One of Chicago's most respected DJ's is here to show you a great time! He's
widely recognized through out the Mid-West, but has played world wide!
Combining feel-good House vibes with funky disco has proven his skills on
the decks.

Shan Tran - Lexington, KY ~(Subliminal Criminals /
ToyMaker Prod.)
With her rare selection of tracks, she makes her own unique sound, dark,
hard and dirty. Never playing the top ten and always going deeper, and
heavier makes her a one of a kind Drum and Bass DJ.

Chris Grant - Milwaukee,WI ~(Outlawed Prod.)
Finishing up at UW-Milwaukee, Chris can be found at underground events all
over the place. Tearing up floors and burning the House down. The founder of
Outlawed Productions is here to show you a good time.

DJ Geist - Champaign, IL ~(ToyMaker Prod./ WEFT 90.1)
This mid-west junglist soldier has put on his own college radio show,
countless mix cd's, and spun at clubs and parties for over 6 years. He likes
the bass to hit your chest like a bowling ball on a windshield, but he also
likes to dig deep in the crates for the tunes that help make a set take you
somewhere higher. Expect constant pressure and raw snarling beats as Geist
salutes the 'Hardcore jungalists out there!

Mr. Miyagi - Chicago, IL ~(ToyMaker Prod. / Audio
Intellect)
Hard...uplifting...dark...filthy....don't think these words belong together?
Then you haven't caught a hard trance/nrg set from Mr. Miyagi. No, not
hardstyle!! Driving, pulsating, acidic, intoxicating hard trance and NRG
that will slam you while it elevates you. Warning : not for arm-crossers or
wallflowers.

DJ Zeek - Bloomington, IL ~(Intergruv Net. / Hotmix 3
records)
Simply put, This DJ rocks the House. With an energetic uplifting style,
always an eye on the crowd, be it opening the night, midnight or sunrise, He
always brings heads to the dance floor whenever he takes over the tables!

Impact - Central, IL ~(ToyMaker Prod. / OGSF)
Behind the decks, ImpacT has always pushed and refined his sound, no matter
the details, the sound has always been house. His precise, quick mixes, ear
for great records, eye for the dance floor and friendly personality have
made him a crowd favorite.

Tim Fusion - Chicago, IL ~(Intergruv Net.)
The backbone behind the CaveMan events down in Southern Illinois is here to
show you a good time. He's loved by all for his blend of uplifting Global
Trance & Techno music. It doesn't take long to see what he is made of. So be
ready for a kick arse set.

Pocketchange - Bloomington, IL ~(C.I.A. / Day 4
Records)
He is one of the original members of the Central Illinois Alliance and
Resident DJ at Paulie's in Bloomington. He has eleven years experience
behind the decks. Pocketchange drops smooth minimal tech-house, techno , and
electro. His genre hopping style is sure to keep you waiting for the next
mix.

K Funk - LaCrosse, WI ~(ToyMaker Prod.)
What's not to say about one of our sweetest cats of ToyMaker Music. She has
never been a let down at any show. She's always full of life, and friendly
vibes. Most of all her uplifting sets have made her one of ToyMaker's top
DJ's.

Dj Vamp - Bloomington, IL ~ (Alien Elements Prod. /
Power 92.9)
Eric has been rocking central Illinois for 7 years now and has stood the

station. Bringing house music to the masses all over central Illinois and

Tom Foolery - Carbondale, IL ~ (Spoolo Life / ToyMaker Prod. / Catalytic Souls) Bringing the ever evolving sound of big room breaks to EDM arenas across the country. Tom relentlessly redefines the notion of breakbeat in the US. Come hear what makes him one of our most in demand breakbeat Djs.
