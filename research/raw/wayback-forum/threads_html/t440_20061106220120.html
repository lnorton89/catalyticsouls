Posted: Sat Aug 26, 2006 9:26 pm    Post subject: What is it?
I'm sorry, tomfoolery's explanation (although all true) did not even begin to explain what Cavestock is. This will be my 4th festival at Shawnee, so take my word for it, wherever you are from, its worth every minute of the drive. As soon as you get into Shawnee you feel the vibes. It's mostly really good people there, and although most are complete strangers, you feel like you know everyone. Don't think of this as just a concert or a festival. It's not. It's an experience. And so far its been one of the best experiences of my life. The last festival I went to there was amazing. And in those 3 days I came to understand more about myself, life, and my friends than I have in 21 years. All I can say is Karma. It's an important thing there, at least I would like to think so. Check this out, on the last day I was there, I was walking down a trail with some friends. I was leading the way (like usual) when my friend, Caleb, told me to stop. I looked back to see that I had walked right past the bigest coperhead I have ever seen. Less than a foot had been between me and the snake. It could have freak killed me. He was all coiled and ready to strike. Later someone asked me if I killed it. I told them I let him live, and it they seemed like they thought I was crazy. I know, maybe I should have killed him, but in my eyes that would have been wrong. The snake spared my life, why should I take his? Karma. All I'm saying is go. Do this, no matter how far it is, or how long it takes. It's worth it, trust me. I can't wait.
