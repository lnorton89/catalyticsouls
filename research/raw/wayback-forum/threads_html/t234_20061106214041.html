Well first off the event was great!! I was impressed with all the acts. I got most of them to sign flyers forme!! yeh!!



On Friday we got there around 5 or 6pm drove down that hill, man its still a b especially in a van full of equipment!! We unloaded everything and I got the lineup for the stage I was playing at, which was at 8, but was of course pushed back to 9 because of the infamous sound problems at the begining in the outlawed stage. Spacehead from STL played before me, jungle, he had a mc that was throwing down shite when I told him I was gonna play gabber/hardcore he got really happy and said he was gonna start a pit and everything. Well it went off great with 2 sound problems as always during my set. The mc cotino estez, (i cant read his writting) was ripping up so hard metal type lyrics for the metal/hardcore mixes i did!! I loved it!! Then Deadman went on after me, I heard a bit of his set then I ran up the lower hill to the main stage to check out the groove. Tom Foolery was playing, some breaky type stuff it sounded good, then Switchfocus came on and they were off the chain hard techno. After switchfocus was Infected Mushroom, well needless to say they rocked!! They had a live guitar which was amazing as well. Then I cant remember who played but it was some housey type stuff so I ventured off to the outlawwed stage yet again to hear more d&b/experimental. so skip forward to saturday night, with Monk who always plays a flawless set of hard techno and whatever else he can throws in. Then John B. from the UK , sic d&b Then Paladin 5.1 with his dark trance I liked it , always a crowd pleaser. Then Electric Skychurch ahh yeh well I must admit I am still singing some of their songs. ayyy yehyehyehya. the other stages were ok but i think 4 should be the maxx stages at the event. Early in the morning sunday it sounded really loud as I was trying to catch some rest in the van. Great to see some of the memphis people representin'!

I would estimate about 2500 ppl attended. Nothing like a bunch of sweaty midwest folx to throw great vibe/energy for the whole weekend!! Plus for future reference, The Apple Tree Inn is by far the most laxxed hotel we have ever stayed in, check out whenever, check in early.. it was great!!

Cant wait to do it again this year maybe or in the coming years!!

Posted: Thu Jul 06, 2006 6:35 am    Post subject: still buzzin...
I am still buzzing from this party. I've been to some absolutely memorable events, but this might just be the best experience I ever had. Worth then entire 4,300 mile road trip me and my buddy shpu3 took from los angeles. I almost didn't bring my fire poi with me but at the last second we decided what the hell. We get there, and there were what like 50 people all with fire dancing materials? Unbelievable!

And that leads to insane dancing with freakin red flames during Electric Skychurch's set... which leads to learning how to eat, capture, and lick fire right smack in the middle of the dance floor.

I have a ton of pictures actually, and a couple of movies, but it seemed like a million people were taking pics of us doing all this stuff, and I don't have any pictures of red flames on my camera.

One more quick story:

I'm sitting on the ground, playing with the fire-swallowing torches with a group of like what 10 other people, I swear it's like we are 6 years old playing pass the hot potato. I look up and I see a seriously stern, mean, red-meat-killing and eating security guard with a mustache, the kind of guy that looks like he didn't just vote for GW but campaigns for him. I'm thinking, "Uh oh, he's gonna bust us for doing all this fire stuff right here in the middle of a crowded area." So, I'm feeling so damn good, I look up at him, and I give him a little smile and a thumbs up (like a question, "Is this okay?") and the dude smiles back at me and shakes his head.

Seriously, in what other party does a fully-dressed security guard not only allow the partyers to play with fire, but stands there watching and enjoying with the rest of the crowd??????!!!!???

Haha anyways the couple of movies I got from the party are going to be fully up on my myspace, and I would encourage any of you to add me to your friends if you have a myspace. Me and shpu3 had such a ridiculously amazing time we are planning on coming back next year, or at least trying to find a comparable party in the midwest at least once a year. I love my hometown of Los Angeles, but hot damn if I don't have the best freakin times of my party life in the midwest. Hillbilly ravers are the freakin best!

I've checked those other picture sites, I can't find any of the people we were hanging with or the red flames, but i KNOW people were coming up to me showing the pictures they were taking of us, so if anyone knows of any other picture galleries post em for everyone to see already.

until next year...
