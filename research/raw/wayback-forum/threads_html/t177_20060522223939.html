The Original Winter Wonderland,
Source 12 yr anniversary show
Saturday December 17 Chicago IL 2005

It's been a surprising year for all of us. I think the last time we
were this surprised was in 1994, when we threw our first Wonderland
event. Shy FX and Skibadee took control of 2000 people on the dance
floor and revolutionized Drum and Bass in Chicago; the Stickmen from
Toronto had everyone making the dance floor Shake at once, and when
Mark Farina brought the true sound of house music. When Ripe, Sense,
Core innovation, Incredibeats, Newtype +, Undergound Elements, Drop
bass network, Alien touch, Vibeanouts, Vibe Alive, Njoy and ATP came
out and represented.

Fanon Flowers LIVE PA-
Black Nation, Mechanism Industry
One of the most respected techno dj's/producers returns to the Midwest.
Fanon Flowers was born and raised in Kalamazoo, Michigan, exactly
halfway between Detroit and Chicago, the birthplaces of techno and
house music. You will be mesmerized by his ability to create brilliant
techno, acid, and house music. Don't miss this rare exclusive live PA.

Lenny Dee and Delta 9
Industrial Strength New York/Chicago Psychotik
The hardcore kings of the US collaborate to bring you a night of
terror, both of them having countless releases on major labels. We
collaborate two of the biggest Hardcore dj's in the world. The
originators of US hardcore you pussy motha freak!!


DJAX Reunion

Steve Poindexter

popular anthems such as work that mother freak and computer madness.
Be prepared to experience one of Chicago's greatest producers and dj's
behind the turntables.

DJ Skull
One of the hardest working producers originally known for his track

bring back an icon in the techno community.

Stacey Kidd will be a part of one of our events. Come witness one
Chicago's most talented house dj's in the city.

Mark Verbos and Submerge 101
New York,Chicago Simple Answer,Hybrid,Psychotik,Distorted
Playing on the decks for the first time, these 2 are responsible for
putting hard Chicago techno on the map! Both of them playing abroad and

Techno for the first time ever.

Mark Almaria and Paul Anthony
Dirty Fabric, Nocturnal, Ammo Chicago

Paul Anthony and Mark Almaria. We decided to put two of the most

won't want to miss.

Entro and Bio Booster

Madgroove
