Featuring Performances by
Wilhelm K- Techno- NYC
The first big name to emerge from out of Indiana, this guy has been throwing events since the early to mid 90's in INDY. He then moved to Detroit and then NYC. His annual b-day parties (the Get Freaky parties) are now a legendary part of midwestern rave legend. He has numerous presses out and represents a variety of labels.
DJ NITRO- House- St. Louis
DJ Nitro has quickly become one of the rising stars of the Midwest. Starting out at after-hours parties in St. Louis, Missouri and working his way through club nights at local clubs and second-room time slots, Nitro built his following from the ground up. It was only a matter of time before he played his first out-of-town show in Muncie, Indiana and, in his words, "It was all down-hill from there."
w
DJ Booth- House- Louisville
The day I quit having fun mixing is the day I quit mixing Fun.. This 3 letter word summarizes DJ Booths styl, personality, and career. Whether a multithousand person event in San Francisco.. or a Sushi Bar/Lounge in Cincinnati nearly 10 years of DJing experience shines through when DJ Booth drops his house beat. You are sure to find smiles, laughter, and the all around good vibe that promoters of all kinds are trying to create.
3D- Drum & Bass- Chicago
3D has been rocking the decks since the early 80's. Labeled the Godfather of Chicago Drum & Bass, he has released more tracks than any other stateside producer and continues to pound only the hardest of beats and the heaviest of bass upon dance floors across the U.S.
Diskokitty- House- Chicago
Diskokitty, also known as Margot Rudy started D.j.ing in the mid 90's @ Southern Illinois University. Since then, she has held residencies at The Mashed Potato Club and Club Mercury in downtown Chicago. She has also spun at various raves in Chicago and Las Vegas. With an arsenal of styles ranging from jungle to various forms of house, this woman is not to be taken lightly behind the wheels of steel. Quite simply A MUST SEE!
w
Avex Axiom- Intelligent Techno- Chicago
Adam Haydock (aka Avex Axiom) is a DJ/producer from Chicago. It was only a matter of time until he became a DJ/producer through his creative ideas, his love for electronic music, and his driving passion for achievement whenever it comes to what he wants to make happen. His sound can range from a dark and defiant sound to a fun funky energetic sound, building intensity and gaining momentum into a storm of tribal percussions, crisp strings, and mysterious industrial sounding synths. whether he starts dark and mysterious or fun and funky, he always builds a positive ascending rhythm, giving drama, mood, and suspense to the audience, Building up to a climatic epic like finish with tracks the crowd cant get enough of.

Directions
From Indy/Columbus/Dayton
Take I-70 west to exit 11, proceed up State Road 46 west (away from Bloomington/Riley), and then follow the red-zone directions.
From Chicago
Take U.S. Hwy 41/Indiana State Road 63 South. When the two highways split, take IN S.R. 63 South. When they rejoin in Terre Haute, take a left at the first light (Maple Ave.) and follow the blue zone directions.
From Louisville
Take I-65 North to I-70 West. Exit 11 on I-70, proceed up State Road 46 west away from Bloomington/Riley, and follow the red zone directions
From Cincy
Take I-74 West to I-465 North to I-70 west exit at exit 11, proceed up State Road 46 west (away from Bloomington/Riley), and follow the redzone directions.
From St.Louis / Kansas City
Take I-70 East across the Indiana border. Exit at exit 11, proceed up State Road 46 west (away from Bloomington/Riley), and follow the red-zone directions.
From Bloomington Indiana
Take Indiana State Road 46 West to the I-70 junction, proceed up State Road 46 west (away from Bloomington/Riley), and follow the red zone directions.
From Ft.Wayne and Eastern Michigan
Take I-69 South to I-465 West to I-70 West. Exit at exit 11, proceed up State Road 46 west (away from Bloomington/Riley), and follow the redzone directions.
From Western Michigan and South Bend
Take U.S. Hwy 31 South to I-465 west to I-70 West exit at exit 11, proceed up State Road 46 west (away from Bloomington/Riley), and follow the red zone directions.
From Lafayette Indiana
Take U.S. HWY 231 South to I-70 West. Exit at Exit 11, proceed up State Road 46 west (away from Bloomington/Riley), and follow the red-zone directions.
From Evansville, and Western KY.
Take U.S. hwy 41 North to I-70 East. Exit at exit 11, proceed up State Road 46 west (away from Bloomington/Riley), and follow the red-zone directions.
From Peoria/ Bloomington IL/ Champaign-Urbana/ Danville IL
Take I-74 East across the Indiana border to Indiana State Road 63 South (possibly the first Indiana exit), once you reach the first light in Terre Haute (the U.S. Hwy 41 junction) take a left, and follow the Blue Zone Directions. 
From Carbondale and other points further South.
Take I-57 north to I-70 East. Once in Indiana, exit at exit 11, proceed up State Road 46 west (away from Bloomington/Riley), and follow the Red Zone Directions.
