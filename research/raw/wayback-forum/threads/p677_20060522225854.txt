Went to Caveman 2 last year, Freakfest last month, and the Aestival Festival this past weekend. IMHO, any party at the cave is a party not to be missed! Freakfest has to be the best so far, both for the music and the vibe. But I gotta admit, even though I'm not the biggest trance fan around, and the fact that turntablism seems to be much more of an art form and therefore much harder to master, I was totally blown away by all the sets I heard this past weekend. Yes, the tix were a bit pricey, but the tribal beats that were goin on up at the main stage all night was well worth the price. Very, very few people were on their asses, everyone was dancing - and that's what it's all about.
