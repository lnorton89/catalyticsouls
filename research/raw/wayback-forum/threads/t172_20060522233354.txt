The U.S. acension of FACTORe is often attributed to his custom blend of 
impeccable studio skills, charismatic performances on the decks, and the 
business savvy that has made his record label, 20k Freaks the first true
trans atlantic label to offer duel pressings in both the U.S. and the U.K. 
simultaneously. His collaborative effortswith the Autobots, Aquasky vs 
Masterblaster, Backdraft, Breakfasters, Deep Impact, and Panic Room 
account for just a taste of his over 60 releases to date. More details at http://myspace.com/factore 
Unkl Ryan (Fried Wax Rec, PM Pro)
A long time fixture of the Dirty South electronic scene, Unkl Ryan is now more likely to be found manning a studio console than the decks. But don't let this new more mature side of Unkl Ryan fool you, he is still a mad man on the turntables guaranteed to get any floor moving with his peak hour stylings.
Dave Skeezy (CatalyticSouls, DnB Science)
Since his first demo hit the streets Dave Skeezy has been in high demand. A junglist to the core, Dave Skeezy puts his soul into every mix. With a touch of turntablism for flare Dave Skeezy's music goes right to the heart of the dance floor.
TomFoolery (CatalyticSouls, Solutions, ToyMaker)
For all that have experienced him, the name TomFoolery is sure to conjure up images of dark and heavy basslines. Moving through flavors of progressive, electro, & funk into all out drum'n'breaks, TomFoolery always has just the right track at hand to bring any crowd into the realm of the nu skool.
