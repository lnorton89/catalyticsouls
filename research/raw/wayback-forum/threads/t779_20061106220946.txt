 BarnYard Boogie 5 
-Til TheCows Come Home... 
Date: November 4th, 2006 
Time: 8 PM to 8 AM

Well, here we go, again! Bringing talent from all over the country, this 
event is not to be missed! Over 30 DJs will be playing. ToyMaker has secured 
a venue near PEORIA, IL with not one, but two sound stages!!! This event 
will no doubt surpass the previous four events and set a new standard for 
ToyMaker events. It will be a 12 hour event, starting at 8 PM and ending at 
8 AM, on November 4th 2006. With state of the art sound systems and lighting 
powered by Outlawed Productions, Maximum Strength, ToyMaker, and many more. 
Call the info lines at 262-364-3124 or 217-215-4091 for more information, or 
visit 
 www.ToyMakerMusicPromotions.itgo.com . 
You can BYOB at this event but, absolutely NO DRUGS, NO MARKERS, NO WEAPONS 
And NO BAD VIBES! And as always water will be free, but a limited quantity 
will be available. So bring some to share as well. Feel free to campout at 
this event, especially if you plan to drink. We would rather you stay than 
drive home drunk. So be smart, and do the right thing. At this event P.L.U.R 
and R.O.A.R will be in full effect. Please respect the venue and our scene. 
Take this opportunity to enjoy what brings us all together -MUSIC- Be safe 
and well see you there.

 Lineup: 
 Avex Axiom - Chicago, IL~(Blueline records / zuvuya 
records / Puzzld Music / Yin Yang / IYS/MNL) 
With his adaptation of EFX processors, a loop factory, and his three deck 
sessions mixed in harmony and with perfection; Avex Axiom has been storming 
through the United States and abroad, playing high profile events such as 
the WMC, Movement, Burning Man, and Caveman. Avex Axiom has also been 
recognized on over 50 radio stations including XM radio, and featured on 
countless websites. Recently Avex has ventured out to Germany for a few 
events as well as prospecting to move out to berlin and moving forward his 
music career.

Psychotik Records)

No introduction must be made for the techno consanguine, as he represents 
the ultimate in energetic, hard but funky house and European techno. This 
will be an exciting year for one of Chicago's leading techno artist as he 
releases several tracks with remixes due this year by, Robert Armani, 
Hyperactive, Bam Bam and Mark Verbos. He has been heralded as one of the 
best up and coming techno acts by many respected and known artists such as 
Richie Hawtin, T-1000 and many others.
