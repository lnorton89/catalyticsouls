My name is Benji Ramsey. I focused on DJing in Sounthern Illinois in 99-01 before moving to Indianapolis. I still DJ off of my laptops, but focus mainly on VJing, lighting and lasers now. I have primarily played out in the midwest until recently compacting my rig for travel so that I can expand my horizons and see the US. I have played alongside most headliners to cross the midwest in the last year such as Oakenfold, Hernan Cattaneo, Icey, Reid Speed, John B,Swamp. Surgeon, Baby Anne, Andy Moor (tilt), D:Fuse, Dara, ODI, Starscream, Paolo Mojo, Gabriel & Dresden, Boy George, Tomislav, Ak1200, Sneak, Soul System, as well as Monk and Berkeley (Hallucination on Film).
I bring a cerebral element that allows people a chance to see inside my head and the crazy amounts of data that are manipulated, twisted, and spit out at the enormous projection screens.
Please contact me to take your event to the next level.
