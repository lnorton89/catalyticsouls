Guess anyone who knows me knows I've been through a pretty traumatic experience recently. I've never had a panic attack in my life, and now I have found myself, after 44 years of always "keeping it together", suddenly hyperventalating, crying, and basically being a basket case anywhere from 10 minutes to an hour or so, a couple times a day. Used to, if i had a "blue" kind of day, I would put on Bob Marley's "Three Little Birds" and pull myself out of the blahs. And you know what? It seems to be working it's magic once more. I haven't had an attack since yesterday, just by listening to it... it's a very calming, beautiful track, Bob's best, IMHO. When Bob sings, "Don't worry 'bout a thing, Cause every little thing is gonna be alright", you just BELIEVE him and instantly feel better. I know I have.
And then there are those times when I don't feel anxious or depressed, I'll just feel this ANGER over recent events. Then I listen to Prodigy, Garbage, and NIN... it's a good, therapeutic way to blow off some steam, and it's sure a lot more safe than owning a gun. Music = Therapy, and it's a helluva lot cheaper than seeing a shrink!
