There are is no "caveman experience 3" being planned for that venue, Shawnee Cave Amphitheatre, at this time. Disclaimer, I am not in anyway affiliated with Intergruv and answer this on behalf of the venue not Intergruv Networks. 
That being said, CatalyticSouls will be hosting a large scale electronic music event at Shawnee Cave Natural Amphitheatre (just outside of Carbondale, IL for those that do not know) No details other than the date are scheduled to be released before CaveFest. But trust me, it will be worth clearing your calender.
