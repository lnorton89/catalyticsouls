There was no anti-house movement here, it just so happened that the genres were listed that way. Several of these DJs play house and other genres. Some may even do several styles within the same set. We will not hold anyone to the styles listed, the DJs are free to play whatever they think is best for the moment. So if you want house, feel free to let people know it.
That being said CaveFest is not an electronic event (per se) The Beyond the Moon stage is more like an event within an event. We also have a really great band line-up happening simultaneaously. There will be a wide range of people with all different tastes so please come out and see if you can't find something you like.
