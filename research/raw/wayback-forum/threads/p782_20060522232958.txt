October 21-22 2005 @ SHAWNEE NATURAL AMPHITHEATER "A Tribute To THE SPIRIT OF OZZ FEST"
The show will feature The Ozzy Osbourne Tribute Band- BLIZZARD OF OZZ. This Chicago-based band covers the music of both Black Sabbath and Ozzy Osbourne, with some deep cuts added to the "hit list" to keep the show interesting to serious fans who tire of hearing "War Pigs" for the millionth time. For the complete Ozzy experience you wont believe this quartets stage presence and energy. www.midwestbeat.com/ezine/july%202004/ozz.htm 
Perris Recording Artist DRIvIN' RAIN will headline both nights. DRIvIN' RAIN was formed in the winter of 1994 in Cape Girardeau Missouri. Since that time they have become one of the midwest's favorite bands. Doing extensive touring throughout Missouri, Minnesota, Illinois, Tennessee, Arkansas, Kentucky, and other midwestern states. They have built a large and loyal following of friends along the way! Since the bands birth in 1994, DRIvIN' RAIN has done extensive touring and performing on the same stage with the likes of Jim Dandy's BLACK OAK ARKANSAS, SAXON, NAZARETH, EDDIE MONEY, John Kay & STEPPENWOLF, TORA TORA, HEAD EAST, XYZ, EVERY MOTHER'S NIGHTMARE, MOLLY HATCHET, PAT TRAVERS, REVIS, RON KEEL & IRONHORSE, CONQUEST, LEE OSKAR (of WAR), RUFUS THOMAS, JOHNNIE BOLIN & FRIENDS, BLACKFIRE, REBEL TRAIN, NO ONE, HELLBOUND, PAT SAVAGE BAND, THE JIMMIE VANZANT BAND, DUST FOR LIFE, DIRTY DEEDS, TALK DIRTY, DRAW THE LINE, CRAZY TRAIN, BLIZZARD OF OZZ, THE TOMMY BOLIN TRIBUTE BAND, and others. DRIvIN' RAIN members have also hung out with some of the biggest names in Hard Rock and Metal. With over 10 years of non stop rock, DRIvIN' RAIN is a valor member of the School Of Hard Rock. In 2004 DRIvIN' RAIN unvailed their first full length 13 song CD. The s/t CD has got great reviews from around the world. The new DRIvIN' RAIN cd is now sold world wide by PERRIS RECORDS. www.drivinrain.net 
EMACIATION will play at 7:00 on Saturday. The concept of Emaciation was spawned in early 1996 . Missouri based avant garde rock/metal group, " Gikk " had started in the early 90's & wanted to expand to music of a more extreme nature . Gikk members Kirby Ray, & Stu " The Immortal " Faris, focused on more brutal back to basics songs mixed with modern forms of metal , to regain the magic & power . Having grown up on every style of 60's, 70's, 80's, & 90's metal & hard rock, & being a founding member of the band Sinister(US) as guitarist in 1984, Kirby Ray had realized this was his lifelong goal . Also to work on several concepts on aspects of the band from the lyrics to the live show . The songs deal with topics such as the paranormal, historical, & things we deal with a daily life . Starting with very few shows a year & going through over 20 member changes, Emaciation has been fortunate to have made many friends in all styles of music . Live, you'll see the theatrical experience of the show, menacing stage presence, corpse paint, spikes, leather, & swords ! Power, Classical, Old School Metal, Death, Thrash, Black, Glam Rock, Ambient, Gothic, & Rock are all influences of the band . Emac prefers to be classified as Thrash Glam Metal . Emaciation has been invited to play at many of the world's best metalfests such as Dynamo festival, Wacken , Black Winter Day, & Milwaukee Metalfest . Emac was voted one of the top six bands of the annual City Of Roses Music Fest in Cape Girardeau, MO by the ticket holders to the yearly outdoor event . Bands they have appeared with are the likes of : Hypocrisy , Nuclear Assault , Enslaved , Mayhem , Cannibal Corpse , King Diamond , The Misfits , Death Angel , Exodus , Destruction , Arch Enemy , Usurper , The Chasm , Zyklon , Opeth , Children of Bodom , Katatonia , The Ravenous , Krisiun , Hellborn , Limbonic Art , Amon Amarth , M.O.D. , Suffocation (US), Windir (Nor), Lullacry (Fin), Naglfar (Swe), Macabre , Anal Blast , Mistweaver (Spain), The Mentors , Incantation , Glen Benton w/ Vital Remains , Malevolent Creation , Gorguts , Ice-T & Body Count , Hate Eternal , Impaler , Eddie Money , Christopher Cross , & many more . The first logo concept was done by Kirby Ray in 97, developed by Emac official artist - Steve Faris in 98, then final logo design done in 1998 by the amazing Christophe Szpadjel of Belgium . Szpadjel is know for the many great logos such as : Emperor, Old Mans Child, Enthroned, Hypnosia, Impiety, Crebain, & Demonic logos .
The Missouri based group has worked with & founded the bands : Steerjockey, The Honey Offering, Gikk, The Dirty 30s, Stage Crew, Blunt Force Trauma, Chemical Billy, Forn, The Underground Lovesound, M.S.I, Lead Circus, Polyduster, Aminion, The Grimshaws, & more. Emaciation is now getting airplay, interviews, positive feedback, & interest from around the globe . We are working to become as finely tuned as possible, gain worldwide distribution, & reach as many fans of music as we can . Thanks to our kin, the bands, clubs, friends, & fans for all your support over the years ! www.myspace.com/emaciation 
 www.emaciation.tk 
Opening the Saturday show is the Missouri based band This, Is A Virtue. A indie/hardcore/screamo band that hails from the Southeast portion of Missouri known as the "Bootheel". Cape Girardeau is the basis of where the band lives and plays. As a band coming from this area we are forced to hear alot of different styles of music, consisting of rock, rap, metal, pop, and lots of country. We strived to be nothing like the "Norm" of this area, and took the inituative to make something different in the area. Alot of the our influeneces are not from these styles, but from the styles that can be heard in the greater St. Louis area, and all over the U.S. as well. These styles contain many of the sub-genres of rock and metal such as hardcore, screamo, emo, indie, and post-hardcore. The live show we bring is full of energy and movement. We play to express our passions onstage. Also the pain, free spirit, and the agression that comes with that. We play to move the crowd as well, so if you are able to attend one of our shows be prepared. In a nutshell, we are just 5 guys who like to play music. Some of us from prior bands, and some not. We are all from a rock/metal background and are just here to LIVE, PLAY, and ENJOY music. www.myspace.com/thisisavirtue
