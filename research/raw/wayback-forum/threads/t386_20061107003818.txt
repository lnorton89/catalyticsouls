I answered a similar question in the UGS 2 thread ( http://www.catalyticsouls.com/forums/viewtopic.php?t=234&postdays=0&postorder=asc&start=0 ). Here's what they asked and I answered: 
They asked:
>> Quote: 
 [quote] In this digital age how does one put their finger on the pulse of the party scene (other than this website)? Are there listserves or mailing lists out there that take the place of old school flyers? I am looking for something a la the Full Moon Parties in Thailand... anyone? anyone? Any help would be greatly appreciated.
I answered :
>> Quote: 
 [quote] Here in ATL the DnB/jungle scene is pretty large, but there is a slightly smaller (150ish people?) "regular" collective that all know each other/post at a site where all party announcements are made for said genre ( www.404audio.com ). There are also broader (all genre) sites that keep us up-to-date on every party in the area (and actually every one in the surrounding states as well). 
I dunno how such runs around where you live, but, there are a few dif. ways to solve your problem : 
1. You could Google your area with other relavent words (DJ, etc) and see what pops up (messageboards, sites, etc.). 
2. You can join the MySpace pages (or look at the actual sites) of your fav. artists and look for tour updates, etc. 
3. You can learn who the Production Companies are in your area and I'm sure some of them have like "E-Vite" or "mailing-list" functions that you can join. 
All of the above are based on the fact that the Production Companies in your area are doing what needs to be done to become successful : PROMOTING their events.
As far as events specifically taking place at the Cave, I'd assume this site would be the one to check for such. Just look on the Fourm Index under the first sub-heading..."Shawnee Cave Events".
