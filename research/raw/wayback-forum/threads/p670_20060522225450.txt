What kind of DJing do you do? I worked at a bar, and just had to fade the songs, not mix, so PCDJ red was fine for that, but if you are doing any kind of beatmatching or anything more complicated than just playing one song after another, I would recommend something else. You could do it on PCDJ red, but it's a pain. PCDJ Red just looks like a dual deck cd player on your screen. You can use it to play music from your computer. It has all the basics like cue and pitch adjust. It feeds the music out of two channels so you can hook them up to two channels on your mixer and do all the cueing, fading and mixing from there.
I haven't used a lot of recording software; just Cool Edit and Sound Forge and they seem to work great. My friend works at a radio station, and they record all their commercials with Cool Edit. Just make sure, if you're recording from your mixer, you have a line input on your sound card. Mic inputs don't like line level signals..... Very Happy
