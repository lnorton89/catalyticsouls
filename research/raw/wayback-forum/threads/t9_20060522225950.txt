He doesn't really. They are bootleg DVD-R copies of material that he released on LaserDisc and VHS for many years. However, he has (to my and many other fans' dissappointment) claimed that "those versions will never be available again", thus forcing someone like me to buy an "illegal" copy in order to have the films I love (unredone) on DVD. It's not that I hate the new versions of the films, that's fine by me, as long as I'm given a choice. While they are cleaned up and have added effects and such to make them more "modern", they aren't the movies I grew up with, which still stand the test of time as far as special effects are concerned. Plus, the new versions are re-edited/dubbed, which changes the intent/outcome of some scenes.
