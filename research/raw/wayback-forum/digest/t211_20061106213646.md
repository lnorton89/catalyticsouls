While keeping in the tradition of musical acts such as Old and in the Way, John Hartford, and New Grass Revival, Cornmeal has continued to push the boundaries of traditional bluegrass music for more than five years. On their second effort, "Tales from Magic Stone Mountain" (2003 Livin' Live records), the band pushes its style further with a blend of 13 original songs recorded at the famed Rax Trax studio in Chicago.
