 Join us as we pay tribute to those who have fought to promote and protect the purity and fun electronic music culture and to those who have made their mark on the continuum of music since the stone ages. Step over to the spiritual side as you enjoy a full on audio/visual, analog/digital experience taking place in a true natural/supernatural outdoor amphitheater unlike anything you have ever seen.
 Imagine yourself at the base of 125 ft. rock wall, inside a 210 ft. deep opening and 140 ft. wide mouth of a cave grooving to the heart stopping beats and blaring rhythms of some of the finest talent ever assembled inside a cave. Located on a beautiful 53 acres right next door to the beautiful Shawnee Forest (So. IL.). The cave, an amazing structure of wonder created over 1,000,000 years ago, faces deep into the Mississippi River Valley.
