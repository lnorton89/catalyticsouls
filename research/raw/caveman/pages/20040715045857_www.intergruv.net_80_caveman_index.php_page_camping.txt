 Camping
 Camping tips 
Camping begins 4 PM Friday July 16th.
Plan ahead thoroughly. Bring more food, more water, more ice, more stuff than you think you will need. No ATM or cash machine on site. PLAN AHEAD!
No ins and outs after Midnight you must bring in all your gear and supplies by then.
Camping is unlimited for this event (Tents only! No campers, RVs or trailers will be permitted)
Please stay on marked trails and pathways. Failure to do so is at your own risk. Theres a reason why they call it wildlife (and were not talkin about the happy hardcore fanatics).
Remember it may be very warm on July 17th Dress and pack accordingly.
Feel free to bring your own mini grill, but no open fires will be allowed. 
You are responsible for maintaining and leaving no garbage or damage at your camping area. Please be courteous and clean up after yourself.
Get to know your neighbors to protect yourselves from mischief and theft.
