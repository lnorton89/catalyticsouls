 Vending 
Check out the many cool things made by hand or not by man. At the Caveman Village youll be able to find artwork thats wearable, usable, or viewable.
If you are interested in vending at the Caveman Experience this year, please contact mattstiffelman@hotmail.com for more information. REASONABLE VENDOR FEE THE MORE THE MERRIER!
 Facilities, Food & Beverages, etc. 
Bring your appetites and thirst. Enjoy a good home cooked BBQ meal, whether youre a carnivore, omnivore, or herbivore. Munch on some tasty hamburgers, hot dogs, or brats. Not into the fleshy foods? We even have a vegetarian menu this year. Cool, refreshing non-alcoholic beverages will also be sold (Did you think we would just sell you food, with nothing to drink?)
Need ice? No worries, you can purchase ice from the old caveman at the main concession booth!
 BYOB 
Alcohol is permitted BYOB for those over the age of 21. Glass bottles will not be permitted. All beverages must be in aluminum cans or plastic containers. Coolers will be searched. PROPER ID REQUIRED. DISTRIBUTING ALCOHOL TO MINORS ON PROPERTY IS UNLAWFUL AND SUBJECT TO ARREST. 
 Security 
NO smoking in parking area * NO open fires * NO swimming in water areas * NO ins & outs after 11 pm * NO drugs * NO bad attitudes * ONLY AUTHORIZED VEHICLES WILL BE ALLOWED INSIDE THE PARK * NO UNAUTHORIZED SOUND SYSTEMS WILL BE ALLOWED 
We reserve the right to refuse admission for any reason 
NOTICE: For environmental safety, flyers for other events ARE NOT ALLOWED TO BE PASSED OUT INSIDE THE GATE. Anyone caught passing out flyers on the property may be subject to ejection without refund and reentry.
Feel free to express yourself and get creative with your presence at the event. We will be hosting our first annual CAVEMAN & CAVEWOMAN costume contest... or just sport the loincloth!!!
