 We proudly present: 
 UnderGround Sound 7 
 The Freaky Tiki 
 at Hogrock Ranch 
 Throughout the years we have seen the evolution of bass music, festivals and society. The past few years have been filled with more innovation and diversity than we have seen in nearly a decade. It is an amazing time to be a part of the scene and surrounded by such creativity. The team here at CatalyticSouls and all of the associated individuals and crews that make UnderGround Sound are ready once again with renewed ideas and creativity to bring you our most exciting show yet. We are once again taking to a new location. We have found a beautiful spot with a friendly land-owner looking forward to hosting all of the beautiful people of our scene. We will be back home in the forests of southern Illinois. This time directly overlooking the Ohio River. The venue has swimming, primitive camping, rv hook ups, running water, and shower houses. We will continue to provide you with a safe and comfortable environment to unleash your creativity along with the production quality you have grown to expect from UnderGround Sound. Grab your grass skirts and Hawaiian shirts and get ready for the world’s largest electronic tiki party: UnderGround Sound 7 THE FREAKY TIKI!

 Description
 Enjoy 3/4 mile of Ohio River frontage in the Shawnee National Forest. We have 115 rv sites with 30amp/120V electric and water. We have boat ramp access to the river and two dump stations. We are open May 1st through November 15th and are just up the river from Cave-In-Rock and down river from historical Old Shawnee Town. Garden of the Gods, Rim Rock and many other beautiful scenic areas are a short driving distance. Come for a weekend, stay for a week, or even longer.
