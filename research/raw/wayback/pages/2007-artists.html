Tomfoolery

TomFoolery is currently one of the most recognized breaks DJs in the Mid-West. Pushing the limits of the breaks sound and defying all notions of what breaks are is routine for this DJ. Coming from the hard and funky sounds of the dirty south into the deep progressive sounds of the Mid-West has left a unique imprint on TomFoolery's track selection and mixing style. A favorite on the underground circuit, TomFoolery will still take time to perform for the older more sophisticated club scene. The past year has seen TomFoolery perform in over twenty cities in twelve states. TomFoolery's big room breaks have landed him in the heart of techno at Detroit's legendary DEMF afterparties, they have seen him follow up acts like Kaskade at New Orlean's Vampire Stripper Sluts and put him peak hour at Tampa's world renown Amphitheater. His life as a master breaks DJ is but one aspect of TomFoolery's appeal. His studio work and fascination with synthesis has begun to flourish. Be on the look out for his name as artist/remixer on productions from around the globe, including the upcoming release of Defiance remixed by top UK nuskool producer, Transformerman. TomFoolery is also behind the scenes designing some of the regions most loved festivals featuring everything from regional bluegrass to some of the world's most esteemed electronic acts. The annual Underground Sound (Mid-West Freak-Fest) parties have received world wide recognition and in short time have developed a cult like following from across the nation and around the globe. TomFoolery is more than just an entertainer. He came up in the light of DJs like Paul van Dyke, Carl Cox, and Uberzone where the DJ was more than a musician but a spiritual guide. TomFoolery has broken free of the constraints around him and seeks to free others: mind, body and soul. Allow him to share the keys of freedom and join him on the journey.

Brian Dervish

I started my music career in 1989, playing in local punk bands in small-town Missouri. I discovered raves in 1994, where I immediately fell in love with DJing and all things electronica. Over the years I have DJed or lugged my computer/keyboard/drum machine (whatever crap I happened to own at the time) to many parties, playing house, breaks, jungle, trance and techno (mostly in that order). In 2002, my wife and I got heavily into the mashup/bootleg scene, and I have been addicted to cheesy pop trash ever since.
