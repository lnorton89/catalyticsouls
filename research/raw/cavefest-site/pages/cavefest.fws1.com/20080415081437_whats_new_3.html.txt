 Volunteers Needed 
UPDATE 
Sunday April 13th... 2:00 PM @ the Cave 
Organizational Gathering 
If you are going to volunteer you need to be HERE! 
Hot dogs, chips and dip and cheese sandwichs will be provided... Bring your own favorite beverage.
We are looking for volunteers to help with security, paramedics, parking, recycling, handing out garbage bags, to help with safety issues, such as informing people to stay off the edges of bluffs, hospitality to greet bands and for cooks to barbeque for the bands, staff and security.... We also need an experienced tye dye T-Shirt Maker..... We will supply the shirts and Supplies.
Anyone interested in volunteering with any of these postions please contact The Caveman (Bob)
618-687-9663
We are also looking for someone to come down and map out the cave grounds. If you have skills in mapping please call Bob @ 618-687-9663
