 Do you have experience? We need your Help! 
 We need you if you have experience in forestry, sign making or an electrician. We are also looking for laborers willing to do some hard labor before the 4-18-08 Show
Here are some of the positions that we need help with!
We need someone to make signs, help clean trails, dig ditches, sand bagging, update and repair electricity & lights.
We also could use some generators........if you could spare one during the concert
