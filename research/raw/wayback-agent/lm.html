Lawmakers, RLC, Quatro's, Marion Lions

  • Jul 12, 2007
  • Jul 12, 2007 Updated Jul 12, 2013
  • Facebook
  • Twitter
  • WhatsApp
  • SMS
  • Email
  • Facebook
  • Twitter
  • WhatsApp
  • SMS

Thumbs down - to the repeated failings by our state lawmakers to prepare a new state budget or address soaring electricity rates that have had voters begging for relief for more than six months. Is anyone in Springfield listening? Does anyone care?

Thumbs up - to Rend Lake College for taking part in The Big Read, and serving as Southern Illinois' sole representative in the National Endowment for the Arts initiative aimed at putting literary books back in the hands of citizens. The college applied for the program and received a $6,500 matching grant that will allow for the purchase of more than 500 copies of Amy Tan's "The Joy Luck Club." The book will be distributed free to interested area residents and a number of community-based activities are planned to promote the effort including book discussions, teas and more. The Big Read kicks off later this summer.
