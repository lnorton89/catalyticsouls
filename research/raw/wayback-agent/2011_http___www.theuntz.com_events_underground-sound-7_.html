What: Underground Sound 7

Since 2005, tens of thousands of people have been a part of the UnderGround Sound experience. 



An independent festival for independent thinkers, dancers, creators and artists, we bring the very best in EDM legends and expose emerging artists to thousands of revelers alongside them, deep in the woods of the Midwestern United States. Combining decades of experience in electronic dance music and festival organization, our team works all year round to create one unforgettable weekend each summer. 



The result: three nights of camping, cutting edge music, pristine sound, and of course our signature visual, light and laser presentation, which has become legendary in its own right. And what better place to experience all that that than back to our home in the forests of southern Illinois, this time directly overlooking the Ohio River. The venue has swimming, primitive camping, RV hook ups, running water, and shower houses: a safe and comfortable environment to unleash your creativity along with the production quality you have grown to expect from UnderGround Sound 7. This year's theme? THE FREAKY TIKI! 



If you are from the Midwest, chances are you’ve probably already been a part of our tradition. To our new friends, we extend an invitation: 2011 is your best chance to experience the weekend that defines the EDM outdoor summer festival.



We are exclusive. We are underground. And if you can take the intensity of a 3 day dance music walkabout, then you are one of us.
