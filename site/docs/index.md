## Read this first

::: warning Tom Hughes
Tom Hughes appears to have died in early 2022. On February 5, 2022, his Bay Area Records collaborator Lo IQ? posted: "A sweet and kind soul has passed Tom Hughes aka Tom Foolery i will miss you." No newspaper obituary was found, but the date fits the paper trail: his Florida LLC filed its last annual report in January 2021 and was dissolved for non-filing in September 2022, and his Facebook page's last post is from February 2021. See [Tom Hughes / TomFoolery](/people/tom-hughes).
:::

**Three framing facts:**

1. **The thesis is a founder's account, written mid-stream.** It documents the first three years of Catalytic Souls (2004–2007) from the inside, as the paper half of a graduate project. It anonymizes rivals and rounds numbers. It was written before the ending.
2. **Almost nothing it describes survives in the form it describes.** The vinyl economy it mourned came back. The download stores it bet on are dying. The cave closed, sat empty a decade, reopened for jamgrass, and is dark again. The all-night camping rave it defended moved to a Missouri campground that the DEA seized under the very statute the thesis attacks.
3. **The author who "had yet to net a profit on electronic events" ended up teaching music production at Full Sail**, the school he couldn't afford in 1999.

## Sourcing conventions

Every page distinguishes three kinds of statement:

- **Thesis says:** the author's own account, treated as a primary source with a primary source's limits.
- **Documented:** confirmed by a dated external source (newspaper, event listing, forum post, company filing, Discogs). Sources are linked inline or in the [findings log](/research/findings-log).
- **Inferred / unverified:** flagged as such in the text.

The Internet Archive was offline for the entire research session, so the old catalyticsouls.com could only be seen through captures one research pass pulled before the outage. The [open questions](/research/#open-questions) list what to fetch when it's back.
