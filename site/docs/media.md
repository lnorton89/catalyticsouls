# Video and flyer archive

Everything audiovisual that survives online from the Catalytic Souls years, gathered September 2026. Videos stay on YouTube under their uploaders' accounts; links go out. Flyers were recovered from the Wayback Machine's copies of catalyticsouls.com and are reproduced as historical documents; rights remain with their designers.

::: tip How to read this page
Titles, uploader, upload date and view counts are as YouTube showed them on September 16, 2026. Descriptions in italics are the uploaders' own words. The raw list is in the repository at `research/raw/youtube/ugs-videos.txt`.
:::
