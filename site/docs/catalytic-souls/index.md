# Catalytic Souls: the crew, the brand, the company

## What it was

Catalytic Souls (written CatalyticSouls on the label and in the LLC, tagline "Evolving Consciousness") was the name Tom Hughes created in early 2005 for his Southern Illinois productions, replacing the 2004 sound-company name Fool'n'Around Sound. The thesis gives the reason: after his Alabama backers pulled out of the summer 2005 electronic festival, "the new name could act as an umbrella for any persons or groups that wanted to be a part of what was happening." It was a crew name, a promoter brand, a website with a busy forum, a digital record label (from September 2007), a Florida LLC (December 2008 to September 2022), and by 2011 "Catalytic Souls Productions." Its own copy in 2011: "a crew responsible for hosting underground electronic and live band festivals in multiple states including the Cave Stock, CaveFest, and UnderGround Sound Series." A bio from the same period calls it "a collective with a mission of evolving consciousness."

The name was explained on the site's front page from its first capture (February 2005) with two dictionary entries: *catalyst*, "one that precipitates a process or event, especially without being involved in or changed by the consequences," and *soul*, all nine senses, ending with "a strong, deeply felt emotion conveyed by a speaker, a performer, or an artist. (Soul music)."

Details: [Underground Sound 1–7](/catalytic-souls/underground-sound) · [Cave Fest & CaveStock](/catalytic-souls/cave-fest-cavestock) · [Label & discography](/catalytic-souls/discography).

**The club side, from the archived site.** Before the first Cave Fest, TomFoolery was already running a monthly at Port Cape in Cape Girardeau (from February 5, 2005, 18+, $3), playing Upstairs Lounge and U Lounge in St. Louis with the Nepol and ToyMaker crews, and booked on ToyMaker's Underground Elements in Bloomington. Fall 2005 brought the touring packages the thesis describes: TechnoMeister ("Dirty South Throw Down," Sept 17), the Face of Bass Tour with Evol Intent (Oct 13–16, St. Louis and Jackson), PsychoTronic with FACTORe (Dec 8–10: Club Europe St. Louis, Liquid Lounge Memphis, The Galaxy Jackson), and a New Year's Eve in St. Louis "coordinating with Dr. Kandee, B&W and Disco Productions." A Mardi Gras night at the Sports Blast followed in February 2006. Psychotronic was still running in St. Louis in 2011.

**Gamma Fest** (August 17–18, 2007) was the crew's first out-of-state festival: a two-night camp fest at Eureka Music Park in the Arkansas Ozarks with Shpongle (DJ set) and Hallucinogen, co-branded with Simon Posford's Twisted Records, "Psymbolic live interactive visual environments," BYOB, RV hookups. The same two headliners returned for Underground Sound 4 a year later.

## The people

The archived Artists page (2007, expanded June 2008) is the only roster the crew ever published. With the thesis and the event bills:

- **Tom Foolery (Tom Hughes)** — founder, breaks DJ, promoter, sound and lighting. The 2008 site bio: "one of the most recognized breaks DJs in the Mid-West... The past year has seen TomFoolery perform in over twenty cities in twelve states... Detroit's legendary DEMF afterparties... follow up acts like Kaskade at New Orleans' Vampire Stripper Sluts... peak hour at Tampa's world renown Amphitheater... He came up in the light of DJs like Paul van Dyke, Carl Cox, and Uberzone where the DJ was more than a musician but a spiritual guide." See [his page](/people/tom-hughes).
- **DJ Andy B** — house. Billed from Chicago on the April 2005 Cave Fest lineup, "CatalyticSouls' house DJ" in the thesis, opener of UGS 2's Friday night to a crowd that grew from 200 to over 1,000. On every Underground Sound bill through 2011, Summer Cavefest 2008, and Cave Fest 2009 after the crew had withdrawn. Site bio in full: "Wishes we would write his bio." Real name not found.
- **Unkl Ryan** — "Is from outer space." From his own MySpace text on the site: "I have been a DJing for about 10 years now, and play many parties all over the south, and Midwest. Its my release for all the shit that builds up in my life on a daily basis." Followed TomFoolery in UGS 2's Friday sunset run; played Stellar Spark IV in Milwaukee on New Year's Eve 2006. Not on the 2009–2011 bills.
- **Brian Dervish (Tekfro), "The Dervish"** — from small-town Missouri; punk bands from 1989, raves from 1994, "house, breaks, jungle, trance and techno (mostly in that order)"; with his wife "heavily into the mashup/bootleg scene" from 2002 (MixDepot). On Summer Cavefest 2008, Cave Fest 2009 and UGS 5, 6, 7.
- **Reperkushin (John)** — jungle and drum & bass since 2001, "from old school jungle sets to new school ragga weaving in hardstep, rollers, jump up, breakcore, and gabber"; "a member of Catalytic Souls and Heavyweight Zoundz"; turning to dubstep and grime in 2008. Billed from Cape Girardeau on the 2005 Cave Fest stage as "DJ Repurkushin." On UGS 7.
- **DJ Derve (Dave)** — "Is not the Dervish." Funky and nu-skool breaks, Cape Girardeau, on the 2005 Cave Fest stage; on UGS 7.
- **Dave Skeezy** — "Is sounding wheezy. (Maybe he should quit smoking)." Billed on the 2005 Face of Bass tour as "CatalyticSouls, Toymaker."
- **DJ Brainstorm** — added to the roster in 2008: sound and lighting for "lock-ins, raves, skate centers" from about 1995, first party 1997, drum & bass DJ, Midwest Drum and Bass Alliance, producing since 2007. On Cave Fest 2008.
- **Spacehead** — breaks, Carbondale, on the 2005 Cave Fest electronic stage; posted the crew's quality-control credo on the 2006 site ("Catalytic Souls believes quality is an absolute need").
- **DJ Fixx** — the only other artist on the label ("The Coast" with TomFoolery, CS001, 2007; the "VIP" mixes of "Control" and "Electrorasta," 2008).
- **Tommy "I.C." Gunns** — on UGS 6 and 7.
- **Chad and Jesse Designs** built the 2005–2008 site; **Benji Ramsey** was the VJ from UGS 3 through UGS 6; **Eric Paxton Stauder (Dots per Minute)** designed the UGS 1 and 2 flyers.
- **Regional stage crews** — from UGS 2 on, each satellite stage was run by a crew from one third of the 500-mile radius. The thesis doesn't name them; the forum did host a "Dust Trails Presents CaveCountry" event and Gettin' Lucky Productions' "Spirit of Ozz."
- **Production partners named in the thesis** — a Memphis laser company, a Milwaukee sound company (the 30-by-7-foot main wall), an Indiana VJ found at a St. Louis-area event, an Alabama rave lighting company (CaveStock 1), Disco Donnie Productions (talent, UGS 1–2).

The April 2005 electronic-stage roster from The Southern shows the network the umbrella covered from the start: DJs from St. Louis, Cape Girardeau, Frankfort KY, Effingham, Bloomington, Chicago and Arizona playing house, breaks, drum & bass, ragga jungle, psy, hard techno and NRG.

## The website and forum

catalyticsouls.com was live by February 4, 2005 as a phpBB portal with eight registered users. By May 2005 the forum had 83 users and 306 posts, with boards for "Shawnee Cave Events," "UnderGroundSound — Mid West Freak Fest! June 18 05," "CaveStock 2," "Club Events" and "House Parties." A redesign in November 2005 added the "Evolving Consciousness" tagline. By mid-2006 the forum had 450 users and 1,028 posts; by November 2006 the member list exceeded 1,400 (many spam accounts, but the thread count is real: the Wayback Machine holds about 2,100 archived threads). The 2006 About page described the site as "your official site for news and updates on CatalyticSouls events as well as your web connection to Caveman Bob and Gettin Lucky Productions," which is to say it doubled as the cave's own calendar. A June 2007 thread was titled "Stabbing at the cave???" (content not recovered). The June 2008 static redesign had Home, About, Forum, News, Events, Artists, Music Shop (UGS 4 presale, will-call $40) and Downloads ("Coming Soon"); it was never updated after UGS 4. In 2010–11 the front page became a single "UnderGround Sound 7 Coming Soon" teaser over a 70-image UGS 6 gallery, with a separate Joomla site at `/ugs/` ("Copyright 2011 Catalytic Souls Productions") selling presales at $80 until April 1 and featuring Shpongle, Bassbin Twins, Starkey and EPROM, with videos of Rabbit in the Moon and Menace to Sobriety live at UGS 6. The domain was parked by May 2013 and has served Japanese antiques spam since 2014.

Recovered page text is in the repository under `research/raw/wayback/pages/` and `research/raw/wayback-agent/`.

## The company

CATALYTICSOULS LLC, Florida, filed December 29, 2008 with a Fort Myers address; manager and registered agent Thomas Hughes. Annual reports 2009 and 2010. Administratively dissolved September 23, 2011, two months after the last Underground Sound. Reinstated December 16, 2019 at an Orlando address; reports filed January 2020 and January 2021; dissolved for non-filing September 23, 2022. Hughes' LinkedIn dated the company January 2006 to August 2011 and described its late form as representing "BayAreaRecords.US, growAglow, and Zcarab through TomFoolery Event Planning, Event Design, Production Consultation, Lighting Design, Acoustic Design, and Audio Production."

## How it ended

No public statement exists. The sequence: Gamma Fest in Arkansas (Aug 2007); last Underground Sound at the cave (Aug 2008); Florida LLC (Dec 2008); withdrawal from Cave Fest 2009; two Camp Zoe years; the Camp Zoe seizure (Nov 2010); one Hogrock year, still advertised on the site in January 2012; dissolution (Sept 2011); domain parked (2013). Hughes' bios led with Underground Sound and Catalytic Souls for another decade.

## What came after it in Southern Illinois

Nothing that can be documented from the web. The research pass on later Carbondale and Murphysboro electronic crews (2010s–2020s) returned no results before the search budget ran out, and the revived Shawnee Cave Amphitheater programs "a little bit of electronic but not too much." Goodale's own 2010 and 2011 shows still carried a "Crash and Burn Techno & Electronic Stage" and a dubstep stage run by Digital-Glitch, which is the last electronic programming at the cave on record. If a successor scene exists, it lives on Facebook and Instagram, where this research couldn't reach. This is the biggest open question for anyone who was there; see [Research](/research/#open-questions).
