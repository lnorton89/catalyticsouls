# DJ & production technology

*The thesis was written as its author learned Reason and Ableton Live, watched CDJs emulate turntables, and argued that laptops had merged the DJ and the producer. Here is where the booth and the studio went.*

::: tip Source
This page is the section "DJ / PRODUCTION TECHNOLOGY" of the industry research report compiled September 16, 2026 (about 80 searches and fetches). Items marked **[Inference]** or **[Unverified]** are synthesis or unconfirmed; everything else links to its source. The [scorecard](/then-and-now/scorecard) condenses it against the thesis's claims.
:::

DJ / PRODUCTION TECHNOLOGY

- **Pioneer/AlphaTheta:** CDJ-2000NXS2 Feb 2016; CDJ-3000 Sept 2020; parent AlphaTheta began branding new products "AlphaTheta" from Jan 25, 2024 (Pioneer DJ name kept for legacy); CDJ-3000X (Sept 2025) first AlphaTheta-branded CDJ with WiFi/USB-C ([MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/news/pioneer-dj-alphatheta-rebrand), [RA](https://ra.co/news/83559), [Sonicstate](https://sonicstate.com/news/2020/09/10/pioneer-releases-the-cdj-3000/)). **NZ Commerce Commission blocked AlphaTheta's acquisition of Serato on July 18, 2024**, explicitly because it would combine the most popular DJ hardware (Pioneer) with the most popular DJ software (Serato); deal abandoned ([ComCom](https://comcom.govt.nz/news-and-media/media-releases/2024/commission-declines-clearance-for-alphathetas-proposed-acquisition-of-serato), [RA](https://ra.co/news/80965)).
- **Software:** rekordbox and Serato are "the two juggernauts"; Traktor "a niche product"/"underdog in 2025" despite Traktor Pro 4 (July 17, 2024) with real-time stems; Traktor was first with stems (2.9, Aug 2015); Serato Stems (2022–23), rekordbox 7.2.8 four-stem separation ([Home DJ Studio](https://homedjstudio.com/rekordbox-vs-serato/), [Digital DJ Tips](https://www.digitaldjtips.com/rekordbox-v7-2-8-adds-4-stem-separation-new-cloud-setup-wizard/), [The DJ Mixtape](https://thedjmixtape.com/virtualdj-stems-vs-serato-stems-vs-rekordbox-stems/)). USB/rekordbox export is the club standard.
- **Ableton:** Live 12 released March 5, 2024; Push 3 standalone (2023) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ableton_Live), [Gearnews](https://www.gearnews.com/ableton-push-3-standalone-studio/)).
- **AI music:** UMG settled with Udio (Oct 2025, per-generation royalties ~$0.002–0.005), WMG with Udio and Suno (Nov 2025); Sony v. Udio and UMG/Sony v. Suno continue; Suno summary-judgment hearing July 2026; AFM sued UMG/WMG over the settlements ([MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/musicians-union-sues-umg-and-warner-music-alleging-member-recordings-were-licensed-to-suno-and-udio-without-compensation-or-credit/), [Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/music/music-industry-news/musicians-union-lawsuit-ai-song-generator-settlement-1236614835/), [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/virginieberger/2025/12/18/launch-train-settle-how-suno-and-udios-licensing-deals-made-copyright-infringement-profitable/)). AI DJ: Algoriddim djay Automix/Neural Mix ([MusicTech](https://musictech.com/news/gear/algoriddim-free-dj-software-djay-pro-ai-automix-and-neural-mix/)).
- **Ghost production:** tracks $300–2,500; producers earning $1k–10k+/month; Mixmag coverage of ex-USSR ghost producers and "My Ghost Market" — no hard market-size figure from an authoritative source ([Mixmag](https://mixmag.net/feature/ghostwriting-in-the-former-ussr), [Kraftbeat](https://www.kraftbeat.com/blog/en/ghost-production/ghost-production-guide-2025)).
- **TikTok:** the four most-popular US TikTok songs of 2023 were all sped-up remixes ([Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/business/streaming/tiktok-2023-report-sped-up-remixes-1235540399/)).
- **Vinyl-only DJ niche:** growing among young DJs (My Analog Journal etc.), EU approved a €20M "Vinyl Revival" programme Feb 2025, but cost barriers noted ([Divert Records](https://www.divertrecords.sk/story/vinyl-only-culture-is-it-elitist-or-just-misunderstood), [DJ Mag](https://djmag.com/content/vinyl-only-culture-encouraging-elitism-electronic-music)).
- **Conferences:** WMC split from Ultra 2011, reacquired by Ultra 2018, held 2019, then a **five-year hiatus**, returned 2025 (Eden Roc) and March 24–26, 2026 (Kimpton EPIC) with an IDMA gala ([EDM Identity](https://edmidentity.com/2025/11/08/winter-music-conference-2026-dates/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winter_Music_Conference)). **ADE 2025: record 600,000 visitors** (from 500k in 2024), 1,200+ events, 300+ venues, ~3,500 artists/speakers; ADE 30 is Oct 21–25, 2026 ([NL Times](https://nltimes.nl/2025/10/27/amsterdam-dance-event-draws-record-600000-visitors)).
