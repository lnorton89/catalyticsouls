# Scholarship since 2007

*The thesis closed by hoping it had "contributed to what appears a steady but slow growing pool of research." That pool became a field.*

::: tip Source
This page is the section "ACADEMIC" of the industry research report compiled September 16, 2026 (about 80 searches and fetches). Items marked **[Inference]** or **[Unverified]** are synthesis or unconfirmed; everything else links to its source. The [scorecard](/then-and-now/scorecard) condenses it against the thesis's claims.

ACADEMIC

- **Dancecult: Journal of Electronic Dance Music Culture** — co-founded 2008 by Graham St John and Eliot Bates, first open-access issue 2009; now published via Huddersfield/Maynooth on OJS; ISSN 1947-5403 ([Dancecult](https://dj.dancecult.net/index.php/dancecult), [DOAJ](https://doaj.org/toc/1947-5403), [Boing Boing 2009](https://boingboing.net/2009/10/01/new-journal-about-el.html)).
- **Key books:** Reynolds *Energy Flash* updated 2008 and 2013 (adds dubstep and US EDM) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Energy_Flash)); Matos *The Underground Is Massive* (Dey Street, 2015) — 19 parties over 30 years, Chicago/Detroit to EDM festivals ([HarperCollins](https://harpercollins.co.uk/products/the-underground-is-massive-how-electronic-dance-music-conquered-america-michaelangelo-matos)); Garcia-Mispireta *Together, Somehow* (Duke, 2023; "stranger-intimacy," Chicago/Paris/Berlin minimal scenes c. 2008) ([Duke](https://www.dukeupress.edu/together-somehow)); *The Oxford Handbook of Electronic Dance Music* (eds. Garcia-Mispireta & Robin James, online from Aug 2021, ~40 chapters) ([OUP](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/35416)). Fikentscher's *"You Better Work!"* (2000) predates the thesis; no major new Fikentscher monograph found.
- **Phylis Johnson:** joined SIU radio-TV faculty 1990, chaired 2002–05, interim chair again 2008; now Emeritus Professor of Sound and New Media at SIU and Professor at San José State; founding co-editor of *Resonance* (UC Press) ([SIU News 2008](https://news.siu.edu/2008/06/062408par8083.php), [SJSU](https://www.sjsu.edu/jmc/about-us/team/faculty/phylis-johnson.php)).
- **The thesis itself:** "Digital Media Technology, Synergy and Making It in Electronic Dance Music" was not found in any web search, and OpenSIUC's search endpoint returned 403. **[Inference]** OpenSIUC's ETD collections largely begin ~2009–10, so a 2007 MCMA thesis is likely only in ProQuest Dissertations & Theses or the Morris Library catalog.
