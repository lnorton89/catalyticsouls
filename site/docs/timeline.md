# Master timeline, 1969–2026

One chronology across the cave, the crew, the author, and the wider industry. **Bold** entries are Catalytic Souls / Tom Hughes. *Italic* entries are industry or legal context. Everything else is the venue.

## Before

- **1969 / 1974** — Gib Todd builds the Shawnee Salt Petre Cave stage; organized concerts from 1974 (Ozark Mountain Daredevils, Steve Miller Band, Head East, Dr. Hook, Tanya Tucker at 15 in 1976, Muddy Waters Sept 17, 1978). Bob Goodale, an SIU student, helps run the 1978 Shawnee Jamboree.
- **Sept 27, 1978** — **Thomas W. Hughes born.**
- **Late 1980s** — Concerts at the cave end. Paddle boats through the 1990s.
- *1994* — *UK Criminal Justice and Public Order Act: "a succession of repetitive beats."*
- **1997–98** — **First Widespread Panic show (Fox, Atlanta); Auburn Hemp Alliance festival; first Phish shows; New Orleans raves.**
- **c. 1999–2000** — **Orlando; can't afford Full Sail; Disney food service.**
- **~2000** — Bob and Lou Goodale take over the cave: "bringing the cave back to the 70s." June 2000 first rock show since the 80s.
- *Aug 2000–2001* — *Disco Donnie's State Palace Theater raided; promoters charged under the crack-house statute; charges dropped.*
- **2001–2003** — **Stagehand for SFX / Clear Channel; club lighting programmer; mix CDs; BA Economics, Auburn, May 2003.**
- *2002–2003* — *RAVE Act introduced (Biden); passes in 2003 attached to the Amber Alert bill; Billings DEA incident.*
- **July 4, 2002** — Nazareth at the cave.
- **Aug 15–17, 2003** — First Caveman Experience at the cave (Intergrüv Networks, Chicago, with Woody McBride): $25, 350 tents, proceeds to Amnesty, the ACLU and EMDEF.
- *Jan 7, 2004* — *Beatport launches with 79 labels.*

## The Catalytic Souls years

- **Spring 2004** — **Hughes arrives at SIU; first cave gig as "Big Dog Disco Machine."**
- **July 16–18, 2004** — Caveman Experience 2: seven stages, Christopher Lawrence, Grooverider, Dieselboy, Diego Ro-K, Doormouse, Woody McBride; Andy B, Space Head and Dervish already on the undercard. **Hughes volunteers; quits drinking.**
- **Fall 2004** — **Fool'n'Around Sound; CaveStock 1** (Daily Egyptian, Sept 30, 2004).
- **Feb 2005** — **catalyticsouls.com's first Wayback capture** (a phpBB forum is running by May).
- **April 15–16, 2005** — **Gemini Moon Cave Fest** (The Southern, Apr 7, 2005: Tom Foolery organizer; Andy B, Spacehead on the DJ stage).
- **June 17–19, 2005** — **Underground Sound 1, "Midwest Freakfest"** (George Acosta). ~1,200 vs 1,500–2,000 projected; big loss.
- *Aug 20, 2005* — *Utah "Versus II" raid; ACLU suit.*
- **Fall 2005** — **CaveStock 2 (profit); four-city club circuit.**
- **Spring 2006** — **Cave Fest breaks 2,000; 1,000 *USAmnesia* CDs.**
- **June 30–July 2, 2006** — **Underground Sound 2: Infected Mushroom, Electric Skychurch, SwitchFocus. The laser names.**
- **Sept 22–23, 2006** — **CaveStock 3 "Shawnee Harvest Fest"** (Chicago Afrobeat Project). Forum member list passes 1,400 by November.
- *Late 2006* — *Jorge Brea founds Symphonic Distribution in Tampa.*
- **April 20–22, 2007** — **Cave Fest '07 Earth Day Gathering** (Vince Herman, Woody McBride, Cornmeal).
- **July 6–8, 2007** — **Underground Sound 3** (Uberzone, Aphrodite, DJ Funk, Reid Speed; 100+ DJs). The Southern reports at least four arrests (July 9–10); three heat ambulance calls; the "Techno Rave" press.
- **Aug 9–12, 2007** — First Gathering of the Juggalos at Hogrock Ranch, Cave-In-Rock.
- **Aug 17–18, 2007** — **Gamma Fest, Eureka Springs, Arkansas: Shpongle and Hallucinogen, with Twisted Records.** The crew's first out-of-state festival.
- **Sept 30, 2007** — **CatalyticSouls label's first release (CS001, "The Coast") via Symphonic; Beatport listing.**
- **Oct 5–6, 2007** — **CaveStock 4.**
- **Oct 18–24, 2007** — **Thesis interviews (Brea, McBride, Stewart). Thesis submitted fall 2007.**
- *2007* — *Satellite Records closes its stores. US vinyl at its bottom (858k LPs in 2006; 990k in 2007).*
- **April 18–20, 2008** — **Cave Fest 4 "The Original 420 Show," hosted by CatalyticSouls.**
- *April 2008* — *First Record Store Day.*
- **June 20–21, 2008** — **Summer Cavefest** (TomFoolery, Andy B, The Dervish).
- **Aug 15–17, 2008** — **Underground Sound 4 "Circus of Freaks": Hallucinogen, Shpongle, Evol Intent, Tommie Sunshine, Baby Anne, Ooah; five stages, ~120 names. Last UGS at the cave.**
- Aug 29–31, 2008 — Strip Mines Music Festival (~550 paid). Goodale's site: "Help Save The Cave."
- **Sept 26–28, 2008** — **CaveStock 5.**
- **Dec 29, 2008** — **CATALYTICSOULS LLC formed in Florida (Fort Myers address); Thomas Hughes manager.**
- **Spring 2009** — **Catalytic Souls withdraws from Cave Fest.** Goodale continues alone.
- **Aug 14–15, 2009** — **Underground Sound 5 at Camp Zoe, Missouri: Bassnectar, Astral Projection, Donald Glaude, EOTO.**
- *2009* — *Dancecult journal's first issue. Pioneer CDJ-2000.*
- **Aug 13–15, 2010** — **Underground Sound 6 at Camp Zoe: Excision, Bro Safari, Rabbit in the Moon 3D.** Last two label releases.
- **Nov 1, 2010** — DEA and Missouri Highway Patrol raid Camp Zoe; later seized by civil forfeiture under the crack-house statute; owner Jimmy Tebeau serves 30 months.
- **April 22–23, 2011** — Goodale's last 420 Show (dubstep side stage). July 8–9, 2011: Caveblast, his last listed event.
- *June 2011* — *Electric Daisy Carnival moves to Las Vegas. Electric Forest's first year in Michigan.*
- **July 21–24, 2011** — **Underground Sound 7 "The Freaky Tiki" at Hogrock Ranch, Cave-In-Rock. The last edition found.**
- **Sept 23, 2011** — **CatalyticSouls LLC administratively dissolved.**

## After

- *2012* — *Skrillex wins three Grammys; Forbes' first "Electronic Cash Kings"; Sillerman relaunches SFX and buys Disco Donnie Presents.* **TomFoolery releases on Bay Area Records begin ("Nothing Left" with Lo IQ?).**
- **Aug 31, 2012** — Cave gate closed, "for sale" sign. **2012 or 2013:** Jeff Parrish acquires it.
- *Feb 2013* — *SFX buys Beatport for ~$50M. Oct 2013: SFX IPO. Aug 31, 2013: two deaths at Electric Zoo; Shelley Goldsmith dies at a DC show, starting the Amend the RAVE Act campaign.*
- *2015* — *Matos, The Underground Is Massive.* **TomFoolery's last Bay Area Records releases (as TomFoolery/Zcarab).**
- *Feb 1, 2016* — *SFX Chapter 11; emerges as LiveStyle in December.*
- *Nov 2017* — *NYC repeals the Cabaret Law.*
- **Feb 2018** — **dubTomFoolery, *listens back* (Orlando).** *DOJ: free water and drug education don't violate the RAVE Act. Ultra buys WMC.*
- *May 2019* — *Beatport LINK: streaming into DJ software.* **Dec 2019: LLC reinstated.**
- **March 2020** — **Hughes' blog: Course Director of Music Production, Full Sail.** *WMC and Ultra cancelled; industry falls 54% in 2020.* *April 1, 2020: Disco Donnie buys his company back.*
- **Oct 31, 2020** — Cave reopens as Shawnee Cave Amphitheater: Yonder Mountain String Band.
- **Jan 2021** — **LLC's last annual report.** **Feb 27, 2021: last Facebook post ("Still love using tapes").**
- **May 28–30, 2021** — Weekend at the Cave (Stringdusters, Sam Bush, Leftover Salmon). July 2021: Blues, BBQ & Soul.
- **Feb 5, 2022** — **Lo IQ?: "A sweet and kind soul has passed Tom Hughes aka Tom Foolery."**
- **Sept 23, 2022** — **LLC dissolved for non-filing.**
- **June 23–25, 2023** — Weekend at the Cave (Greensky Bluegrass, Twiddle, SteelDrivers, Elephant Revival). May 26, 2023: Wade's Daily Yonder interview.
- *Sept 2023* — *Electric Zoo collapses; Bandcamp sold to Songtradr.*
- *March 13, 2024* — *Berlin techno added to Germany's UNESCO intangible heritage list.*
- **April 5–8, 2024** — Total Solar Eclipse Festival at the cave. **June 7–9, 2024** — Shawnee Cave Revival. **July 10, 2024** — Red Clay Strays.
- *July 18, 2024* — *New Zealand blocks AlphaTheta's purchase of Serato.*
- **Aug 6, 2024** — Weekend at the Cave cancelled; venue "closed for the season." No events since.
- *Dec 31, 2024* — *Watergate, Berlin, closes.*
- *2025* — *US vinyl passes $1 billion (46.8M units); IMS values electronic music at $15.1B; deadmau5 sells his catalog for $55M and retires the Cube; Boiler Room sold to KKR-owned Superstruct; Spotify returns to DJ software; WMC returns after five years.*
- *Jan 30, 2026* — *Infected Mushroom, IM30.*
- *April 15, 2026* — *Jury finds Live Nation/Ticketmaster liable on every antitrust count.*
- *June 2026* — *Juno Download shuts down.*
- **Sept 16, 2026** — This wiki compiled. The cave is dark; catalyticsouls.com is a Japanese antiques spam domain; the thesis survives as a .docx.
