## Shawnee Salt Petre Cave / Shawnee Cave Amphitheater (3747 IL-127, Murphysboro IL 62966)
- **Pre-history:** Gib Todd built the venue in the late 1960s ("erected 1969"); acts through the 70s–80s: Head East, Muddy Waters, Tanya Tucker (age 15, 1976 news clip), Dr. Hook & the Medicine Show, Steve Miller Band, T.G. Sheppard. Sources: KFVS Oct 16 2020 (https://www.kfvs12.com/2020/10/16/live-music-return-historic-cave-amphitheater/); Bluegrass Today Apr 5 2021 (https://bluegrasstoday.com/shawnee-cave-amphitheater-to-reopen-for-memorial-day-event/); Daily Yonder May 26 2023 (https://dailyyonder.com/qa-organizing-a-music-festival-underground/2023/05/26/).
- **Goodale era:** Robert "Caveman Bob" Goodale and wife Lou owned/operated it in the 2000s; roots in Centralia IL; ice cream, coal, firewood businesses; reportedly saved the previous owner's life and bought the property cheaply. Source: JamBase, Strip Mines Music Festival Aug 29–31 2008 (https://www.jambase.com/article/strip-mines-music-festival-08-29-31-il); showcaves.com listing (contact "Cave Man Bob", 618-687-9663).
- **Caveman Experience 2, July 16–18 2004:** organized by Tim Fusion / Intergruv Networks with MiddleSchool Productions and The Genius of Fun; 60+ DJs; headliners Christopher Lawrence, Grooverider, Dieselboy; $25/$35; camping capped at 400 tents; all ages. Source: Bluelight thread (https://www.bluelight.org/community/threads/il-myrphysboro-07-16-04-the-caveman-experience-2-salt-petre-cave-review.151589/). (Thesis says Woody McBride was the 2004 backer and a Chicago promoter fronted it.)
- **Gemini Moon Cave Fest, April 15–16 2005** (The Southern Illinoisan, Marleen Shepherd, Apr 7 2005; https://thesouthern.com/entertainment/music/cave-fest-offers-unique-setting-for-warm-weather-music/article_66e2bc83-4525-51f5-8407-3c2ce9dba2b1.html): organizer "Tom Foolery"; ~800 expected; $12/$20; BYOB; www.catalyticsouls.com listed. Band stage: Lyric, MANDAT, Fusion Blue, DreamLab (Pink Floyd tribute), Backwoods Hippies, Dropped Once, Mothership Orchestra, G-String Hypothesis, Jake's Leg, Helping Phriendly. "Beyond the Moon Electronic Stage": Pituko (STL), JTH (AZ), **Andy B (house, Chicago)**, DJ Derve (Cape Girardeau), **Tom Foolery (breaks, Carbondale)**, Shan Tran (KY), Avex Axiom, DJ Kiddo, RHIFT, junglesista101, Digerati, DJ Repurkushin, Jesse Adams, DJ Scoto, Cm3 ft MC Hex Villan, Devo, **Spacehead (breaks, Carbondale)**. Ticket outlets: Plaza Records, Old Towne Liquors (Carbondale), Mungo Jerry's (Murphysboro), BeatNix (Belleville), etc.
- **Underground Sound (UGS) editions (confirmed):**
  - UGS 1 "Midwest Freakfest": weekend of June 18 2005 (venue agent: scottdanesi.com, 9 stages, George Acosta; thesis: ~1,200 attendance vs 1,500–2,000 projected, 8 stages, 150+ artists, $40 door, 18+).
  - UGS 2: June 30–July 2 2006 (thesis + Shroomery thread https://www.shroomery.org/forums/showflat.php/Number/5619178): Infected Mushroom, Electric Skychurch, SwitchFocus.
  - UGS 3: July 7 2007 weekend (YouTube "Underground Sound 3 - 7-7-07 - Shawnee Cave Rave" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NMQOhb7AhHg). Thesis: "Techno Rave" headlines, sheriff complaints, 3 ambulance calls (heat).
  - UGS 4 "Circus of Freaks": Aug 15–17 2008, Shawnee Cave; Hallucinogen (live), Shpongle (DJ set), Skazi; $45 presale / $60 door; "Hosted by CatalyticSouls". Sources: RA event 57997 (https://ra.co/events/57997); Last.fm (https://www.last.fm/festival/496417+Underground+Sound+4).
  - UGS 5: Aug 14–16 2009, **moved to Camp Zoe, Salem MO** ("a new location full of its own natural wonders"); Astral Projection, Bassnectar, Donald Glaude, EOTO, Fort Knox 5, Woody McBride, Keith MacKenzie, TomFoolery, Andy B, VJ Benji Ramesey. Source: Dub 4 Food blog, June 29 2009 (http://dub4food.blogspot.com/).
  - UGS 6 "Crossing the Event Horizon": Aug 13–16 2010, Camp Zoe; Jamie Janover / Nassim Haramein Resonance Project tie-in; Excision, Bro Safari, EOTO, Rabbit in the Moon 3D, Evol Intent, VibeSquaD, ill.gates, Woody McBride, TomFoolery, Andy B, Charles Feelgood, Angel Alanis (26 acts). Sources: Last.fm (https://www.last.fm/festival/1483272+Underground+Sound+6); RA event 182455. **Camp Zoe was raided by DEA/MO Highway Patrol Nov 1 2010; owner Jimmy Tebeau (The Schwag) pleaded to "maintaining a drug-involved premises" (the same crack-house statute as the RAVE Act), 30 months; property forfeited, later Echo Bluff State Park** (https://jambands.com/news/2013/06/13/schwagstock-founder-jimmy-tebeau-heads-to-federal-prison/, https://www.justice.gov/archive/usao/moe/news/2012/october/tebeau_james.html).
  - UGS 7 "The Freaky Tiki": July 21–24 2011, **Hogrock Ranch, Cave-In-Rock IL** (same site as Gathering of the Juggalos 2007–2013); Nigel Richards, Noel Sanger, Keith MacKenzie, TomFoolery, Starkey. Sources: RA event 266968; The Untz listing; Flickr set by Notley Hawkins; thechilluminati.com forum thread (site down at time of research).
  - No trace of a UGS 8 (2012+) was found in any search. **UGS 7 appears to be the last edition.**
- **Cave Fest after the thesis:** Cave Fest April 2008 headlined by Cornmeal (JamBase); Cave Fest April 24–25 2009 — Rollitup thread says Catalytic Souls "initially participated but later withdrew due to unspecified issues" (https://www.rollitup.org/t/cavefest-in-carbondale-il.168943/). Strip Mines Music Festival Aug 29–31 2008 (Zach Swinderman, Tim Roberts; ~550 paid; $30).
- **Shutdown:** Shane Wade (Daily Yonder 2023): "in the 2000s, it became an underground EDM scene, which... kind of got out of hand with some of the partying that the kids down there from the college did. By probably 2008 or 2009 it shut down. My partner acquired it in 2012 or 2013 and then we just let it sit for a while, let the smoke clear... The city leaders had a bad taste in their mouths from that." Bluegrass Today says foreclosure → purchased by Jeff Parrish (prints "2004", which conflicts with Goodale's documented 2005–2008 ownership; Wade's "2012 or 2013" is more consistent).
- **Revival:** Renovated 2020 (bigger stage, drainage, concrete, RV park); reopened Oct 31 2020 with Yonder Mountain String Band (socially distanced); "Weekend at The Cave" May 28–30 2021 (Infamous Stringdusters, Sam Bush, Leftover Salmon, Keller Williams); 2023 Weekend at the Cave (Greensky Bluegrass, Twiddle, SteelDrivers, Sam Bush, Elephant Revival); Shawnee Cave Total Eclipse Festival Apr 5–8 2024; Shawnee Cave Revival June 7–9 2024 (Grace Potter, Samantha Fish, Marc Broussard, Andy Frasco, Paul Thorn); Red Clay Strays July 10 2024; **Weekend at the Cave Aug 30–Sep 1 2024 (Railroad Earth, Galactic) cancelled Aug 6 2024, no reason given, "closed for the season"** (KFVS, WSIL, West Kentucky Star). Songkick/JamBase list **no events for 2025 or 2026**. Wade's stated cap: ~3,000 people ("I like it at three").

## CatalyticSouls / TomFoolery after 2007
- **Label (Discogs label id 134243):** 14 digital releases, CS001–CS108, 2007–2010, all TomFoolery (some with DJ Fixx): The Coast (CS001, released 2007-09-30), Atomic Rock, Dance Sucka, Defiance (Transformer Man Mix) (CS012), MindWrecker (2007); Electrorasta, Control, Jedi Mind, Control VIP (DJ Fixx Mix), Electrorasta VIP (DJ Fixx Mix), Time To Freak (2008); Bring The Bass (2009); BassLion Breaks Vol 1, Face Smack (2010). Raw JSON: `research/raw/discogs-tomfoolery-releases.json`.
- **Later releases (Bay Area Records, 2012–2015):** Nothing Left (w/ Lo IQ?), Space Face Jake (w/ Unter Liëben), Sexy Bitch, The Truth (2012); Let Your Mind Go (2013); Next Level, Locked & Loaded (w/ BBK), Excursions In Tech Vol. 1 (as TomFoolery/Zcarab) (2015). Discogs aliases: Hugues, Zcarab. Remix credits: Anemo "Musicbox" (2008), Nicole Sasser (2015).
- **Business entity:** CATALYTICSOULS LLC, Florida, filed Dec 29 2008; manager/registered agent Thomas Hughes, 7508 Puritan Rd, Orlando FL 32807; admin-dissolved Sept 2011; reinstated Dec 16 2019; last annual report Jan 28 2021; admin-dissolved Sept 23 2022 (https://bisprofiles.com/fl/catalyticsouls-l08000117362).
- **Bio (c. 2011, Psychotronic 5 flyer, Koken Art Factory, St. Louis):** "TomFoolery, based in Miami... founder of CatalyticSouls, a collective with a mission of evolving consciousness... top 5 breaks DJs globally and #1 Acid Breaks per The DJ List." Later bio (sound.radar): "From the beaches of Miami to the sands of the Playa and any number of fields, forests, clubs, barns and warehouses in between... expressed as bass music."
- **Full Sail:** tomfoolerymusic.blogspot.com (last post March 2020): "Tom Hughes, DJ, Musician, Music Producer, EDM Festival Producer... Course Director of Music Production at Full Sail University in Winter Park, Florida"; LinkedIn /in/djtomfoolery "Course Director at Full Sail University". (In the thesis he had hoped to attend Full Sail in ~1999 but couldn't afford it.)
- **dubTomFoolery** (Bandcamp, Orlando): "listens back" (Feb 2018), hardware dub techno "through miles of cable, tons of circuits, & a ghost 32 LE". Facebook page "TomFoolery" (935 followers), last post Feb 27 2021 ("Still love using tapes").
- **Death:** Facebook post by collaborator Lo IQ? (Bay Area Records), **February 5, 2022**: "A sweet and kind soul has passed Tom Hughes aka Tom Foolery i will miss you" (https://www.facebook.com/LoIQMusic/posts/4892018700866939/). No newspaper obituary located via web search. Consistent with the LLC's last filing (Jan 2021) and 2022 dissolution.
