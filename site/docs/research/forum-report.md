# The catalyticsouls.com forum, 2005–2006

*Mirrors `research/raw/wayback-forum/report.md`. The thread index and all 2,089 rendered thread texts are in the repository under `research/raw/wayback-forum/`.*


Compiled September 17, 2026 from the Wayback Machine's captures of the phpBB forum at catalyticsouls.com (root `viewtopic.php` in early 2005, `/forums/` from spring 2005). The scraping agent fetched every archived thread page (2,089 captures) before the archive went offline; `parse_thread.py` rendered them to `threads/*.txt`; `thread-index.md` lists 451 unique topics. The capture dates cluster in May 2006 (661) and November 6–7, 2006 (1,362), with a handful from 2005 and two from 2007. **No threads from 2007 were captured** except a July 4, 2007 Cape Girardeau BBQ listing, so the June 2007 "Stabbing at the cave???" thread and any UGS 3 / UGS 4 discussion are lost.

## Size and shape

- 1,325 unique posts by roughly 250 usernames (spam accounts excluded from the count where obvious; ringtone and pharmacy spam accounts appear from October 2006 and dominate the late captures).
- Top posters: Tangent 133 (Atlanta area; the forum's moderator-in-spirit), tomfoolery 113 (admin), K_I_T 100 (St. Louis drum & bass; extropyenterprises), Guest 76 (mostly Tom before he registered), Dj Derve 38, zesty 38 (Murphysboro; flyer designer; Caveman 2 staffer), Reperkushin 28 (signature "Jon Hamilton"), SpaceHead 27 (Carbondale; Ghetto Puff Productions), thcsyndicate 24, dragonflybee 18, teknohippie 14, yitic 14, Scotty Mac 11, Jayzer 10 (Jordan Welch, Murphysboro), jadams 9 (site admin), Xander11819 9, Lotek 9, Bronco 8 (Michigan), skablam 8, shelby 7 (Quincy, moved to Murphysboro 2004), XBooX 6 (Kansas), GGJim 5 (Forced Entry Records, St. Louis).
- Boards: General/Discussion, Creative Zone, RideShare, Club Events, House Parties, Feedback, Introductions, photos, Event Listings, and one board per festival: Gemini Moon Cave Fest, UnderGround Sound, CaveStock 2, CaveFest06 420 edition, Cave Jam Solstice, The Spirit of Ozz, Grave Dancing, Dust Trails Presents CaveCountry.

## What the threads establish

**Underground Sound 1 (June 17–19, 2005).** The complete bill is in thread 54 ("Flyer and Info," April 17, 2005): main stage George Acosta, Craze w/ MC Sharpness, Doc Martin, Micro, Nigel Richards, Dylan (UK), DJ Lee (Metalheadz), FACTORe, Elektromechanik live PA, Kaos, Evol Intent, DJ Liquid, Bucky Fargo, Aus-10, Quantum, Mary Jane, Wisdom, Diggz, Fitzpatrick, DJ Scoto as Dr. Destructo (breakcore live PA), Drizno, Tom Foolery; a "Beyond the Arch / Midwest Massive Stage" for St. Louis and local DJs (Andy B "Funky House – Cdale," SpaceHead "Jungle – Cdale," Unkle Ryan "Hard House – Jackson MS," Derve and BrainStorm from Cape Girardeau, Tony Foo Yong & Karizma, Dylan Thomas, GG Jim); a "Free Agent Stage" run by Kansas City crews; an "Outlaw Stage" run by Wisconsin and Minnesota crews (The Flashbulb live). Rideshare threads came from Tampa and Michigan. Aftermath: "the lights, music, atmosphere, everything was perfect... listening to Dylan spin DnB as the sun came up was an amazing experience" (yitic, June 28, 2005); Forced Entry Records posted photo galleries and thanked "Catalytic Souls, TFY, Midwest Massive and Disco Donnie."

**The Caveman Experience question (thread 42, March 2005).** Asked about a third Caveman Experience, Tom answered as a guest: "There is no 'caveman experience 3' being planned for that venue... I am not in anyway affiliated with Intergruv and answer this on behalf of the venue... CatalyticSouls will be hosting a large scale electronic music event at Shawnee Cave... it will be worth clearing your calender." The thread is polite; the hostility the thesis describes played out on other boards (Tom links a Bluelight thread in September 2005).

**Cave Fest 2005 and the clean-up problem.** Thread 11 tracks the lineup as it grew (DreamLab's Floyd tribute "designed especially for the Cave," Zacc Harris of Broken Grass with the Mothership Funk Orchestra, MANDAT "masters of the musical journey"); Scott gave Andy B his slot. Thread 61 (April 19, 2005): "Bob (the owner) and his wife were left to clean up a shitload of garbage"; a CaveCleanUpCrew was proposed; a Caveman 2 staffer remembered clearing "a HUGE pile of rotten garbage." Tom: "We have the park about 95% cleaned up now."

**CaveStock 2 (September 23–25, 2005).** Flyers were late because "our associates who did the flyer design got caught in the hurricane in Jackson, MS"; promotion fell back on WDBX and a Nightlife ad; a Schwagstock-goer reported a cancellation rumor, then called Bob and denied it. Tom's stock answer on vehicles: "All vehicle permissions are at the discretion of the owners."

**Underground Sound 2 (June 30–July 2, 2006), thread 234, twelve pages.** The March 11, 2006 announcement is the crew's manifesto ("Throughout history the first to make advances are often cast out by society and referred to as freaks... One year ago we gathered to bring ourselves in line with the cosmic rhythms and sent a message out into the universe that we are ready to advance. Now we come together to receive the response. CatalyticSouls and Disco Productions invite you to join in on this journey to enlightenment."). Bill: Electric Skychurch (Lumb and Morganstern), Infected Mushroom ("we are even flying in the guitarist for IM"), John B, Woody McBride live PA, Monk of Rabbit in the Moon w/ Hallucination on Film, FACTORe, Agent K, Paladin 5.1, Kid Icarus, SwitchFocus, Elephantronic, Gein, Spree & ADB, Alex Pi, Riot Squad, Unkl Ryan, TomFoolery, Reperkushin; I/O AudioVisual offered a live AVPA. Tom deleted an anonymous critic ("we have bigger acts this year... soundsystem will be much improved") and told a 17-year-old "This event is 17+. Be smart and be discrete and you won't have a problem." Capacity was listed at about 3,000. Afterwards: "the best rave in the cave yet" (DJ Odom, Mt. Vernon); "the best time I have ever had in my life" (FiSh, Southern Illinois); "This needs to happen like every 3–4 months" (XBooX, Kansas); "one of beauty, shock, amazement and overall high quality production" (High Lucy Nation, the Nebraska crew that would run a sound camp at UGS 5). Tangent's reply to the Kansas carload is the forum's economics in one line: "the people that throw it aren't making much (if any) money off of it. They do it for the vibe/scene and personal satisfaction."

**Club life.** Tom's 2006 signature: "USAmnesia Dates: Thursdays at The Skyy Box (inside the Sports Blast) Carbondale," plus Terre Haute, Wilmington NC, St. Louis, Little Rock, Cape Girardeau, Louisville. SpaceHead's Ghetto Puff Productions threw "Grave Dancing" (October 15, 2005, Sports Blast) with 3m Productions and sixteen DJs including Intergruv's Tim Fusion; zesty designed the flyer and later admitted "I dont even think anyone checked ID's." Other listings: Gigantor + TomFoolery + Dylan Thomas (October 13, 2005), Barn Yard Boogie (Peoria), PsychoTronic w/ FACTORe (December 2005), Champaigne Super Nova NYE, HeavyWeight Stylez (St. Louis, April 2006), the Spirit of Ozz metal weekend (Goodale's, October 2005, "not a catalyticsouls event").

**Politics.** Tangent reposted Evol Intent's eyewitness account of the August 2005 Utah raid the day after it happened, under the title "Welcome to the Police State known as 'Utah'": "We will not stand for our scene being treated like a terrorist organization!" A "Know your rights" thread followed. This is the incident the thesis cites two years later from the ACLU's press release.

## Gaps

- Nothing from 2007–2008: no UGS 3 or UGS 4 threads, no "Stabbing at the cave???" (June 2007), no move-to-Camp-Zoe discussion. The forum index of June 14, 2007 is the last evidence the board was alive.
- The 2005 Bluelight thread Tom linked (threadid 219014) about CaveStock/Caveman politics was not retrieved.
- Photo threads reference galleries at phocas.net, forcedentryrecords.com and screemnet.com; only phocas.net still resolves.
