## A. THE VINYL-TO-DIGITAL COLLAPSE → THE VINYL REVIVAL

**The bottom was exactly when the thesis was written.** Nielsen SoundScan US vinyl LP sales: 858,000 (2006, the all-time SoundScan low) → 990,000 (2007) → 1.88M (2008, +89%). Sources: [Computerworld](https://www.computerworld.com/article/1567033/back-to-the-future-vinyl-record-sales-double-in-08-cds-down.html), [LA Weekly](https://www.laweekly.com/nielsen-soundscan-2008-sales-figures-released-lp-sales-up-89-percent-and-neutral-milk-hotel-breaks-the-top-ten/).

**RIAA 2025 year-end (published March 2026):** vinyl revenue $1.04B on 46.8M units (up 9.3% from $954.4M / 43.4M in 2024); first time over $1B since 1983; 19th consecutive year of growth; vinyl out-earned CDs >3x (46.8M vs 29.5M units). Total US recorded music $11.5B. Streaming $9.75B; paid subscriptions 106.5M accounts, $6.38B (55.3% of revenue); **digital downloads down 5.9% to $221.8M** (the format that "killed vinyl" in 2007 is now smaller than vinyl by ~5x). Sources: [RIAA](https://www.riaa.com/reports/2025-year-end-music-industry-revenue-report-riaa/), [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/pro/riaa-2025-music-report-revenue-streaming-vinyl/), [Music Week](https://www.musicweek.com/labels/read/riaa-us-revenue-hits-record-high-as-vinyl-sales-pass-1-billion/093770), [MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/us-paid-music-streaming-subscriptions-hit-106-5m-in-2025-adding-6-5m-accounts-yoy-the-strongest-growth-since-2022/). Luminate counts 47.9M vinyl units in 2025 (+8.6%) ([Variety](https://variety.com/2026/music/news/taylor-swift-vinyl-sales-rose-19th-consecutive-year-2025-luminate-year-end-report-1236630636/)).

**Pressing-plant bottleneck 2021–22:** ~300M units on order vs ~150M/yr global capacity (mid-2021); the Feb 2020 Apollo Masters fire destroyed one of two lacquer suppliers; Adele's *30* (~500k LPs) became the scapegoat but insiders blamed majors' bulk orders; indie labels faced waits into Aug 2022 and staggered digital/physical releases. Sources: [Variety](https://variety.com/2021/music/news/adele-vinyl-record-pressing-plant-lp-shortages-1235103951/), [NME](https://www.nme.com/news/music/whats-causing-the-vinyl-delay-adele-is-not-the-problem-say-music-industry-insiders-3094213), [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/music/pop/adele-30-vinyl-manufacturing-delays-9655895/), [CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/lite/story/1.6253630).

**Record Store Day:** conceived 2007, launched 2008 when there were ~1,400 US indie stores; now ~1,600 participating retailers on six continents; RSD 2025 (18th year) sold 1.2M albums in the US in RSD week (just over 1M vinyl), 5th straight year over 1M; UK indie vinyl sales +270% vs weekly average. Sources: [RSD About](https://recordstoreday.com/CustomPage/614), [Luminate](https://luminatedata.com/blog/record-store-day-2025-get-to-know-its-vinyl-buying-u-s-audience/), [Music Week](https://www.musicweek.com/labels/read/record-store-day-2025-delivers-best-weekly-sales-for-indie-retail-in-three-decades/091833). Store counts: ~2,000 US indie shops per Alliance Entertainment ([ICSC](https://www.icsc.com/news-and-views/icsc-exchange/record-stores-are-enjoying-a-modest-revival)).

**Dance-specific retail ecosystem:**
- **Satellite Records** (NY/Boston/Atlanta, 1993–2007) closed all stores in 2007 and relaunched as a curated download site ([Crossfadr](https://crossfadr.com/2012/09/20/satellite-closer-to-the-record-store-experience/)); Atlanta Moreland Ave location listed as closed ([Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/satellite-records-atlanta)).
- **Hard Wax** (Berlin, founded 1989 by Ernestus/von Oswald) lost its Paul-Lincke-Ufer home in 2023 and moved into Kraftwerk/Tresor building ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hard_Wax)). **Phonica** (London, 2003) still operating and opened a Berlin outpost ([Vinyl Factory](https://www.thevinylfactory.com/features/a-definitive-guide-to-berlins-best-record-shops)).
- **Juno Download shut down abruptly in early June 2026** after ~20 years; the physical Juno Records store continues (the download arm had been split off in 2013). DJ TechTools recommends Bandcamp, Traxsource, Beatport as the remaining download options. Source: [DJ TechTools, June 2, 2026](https://djtechtools.com/2026/06/02/farewell-juno-download/).
- Traxsource still operating and "holds up for house and techno" (same source).

**Per-release economics today:** 300 units is the standard indie run (price breaks kick in at 200–300); a 300-unit pressing runs ~$2,265 all-in (2026 guide) and breaks even at ~84 copies at $30; 100–300 copies cost $1,000–3,000; labels report 60% of press costs covered by pre-orders. Sources: [Other Record Labels](https://www.otherrecordlabels.com/vinyl), [Chartlex](https://www.chartlex.com/blog/money/vinyl-pressing-cost-guide-2026), [Vinyl Alliance](https://vinylalliance.org/resources/pressed-for-success-how-to-release-on-vinyl/). **[Inference]** The thesis's 300–600 "collapse" figure for a dance 12" in 2007 is now the *normal, planned* run size, and it is profitable because of pre-orders, Bandcamp and $12–15 12" prices rather than distributor-driven 2,000-unit gambles. No Resident Advisor piece giving current dance-12" averages was found.

**Bandcamp:** acquired by Epic Games March 2022; sold to Songtradr Sept 2023; ~half of 118 staff laid off (60 offered jobs), disproportionately Bandcamp United union members; NLRB charge filed Oct 2023 ([Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/business/business-news/bandcamp-layoffs-songtradr-1235619800/), [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/epic-games-bandcamp-layoffs-songtradr-fortnite-music-1850931222), [MBJ](https://www.thembj.org/2023/11/bandcamp-songtradr-aquisition-leads-to-layoffs-and-a-lawsuit/)). **Bandcamp Fridays** (started March 2020): $154M paid out through 2025; $19M in 2025 alone (record); single-day record $3.8M on Dec 5, 2025; eight dates scheduled for 2026; $1.5B paid to artists lifetime ([MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/bandcamp-fridays-hit-154m-in-payouts-since-2020-with-19m-paid-in-2025-alone/), [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/pro/bandcamp-fridays-2025-total-payout/)).

**Dance vinyl specifically:** Luminate's 2026 mid-year report names Dance/Electronic the largest US growth genre (+0.51 share points), partly on 2015–2017 catalog nostalgia ([Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/lists/luminate-midyear-report-2026/)). No genre-level vinyl unit breakdown for dance was found; Discogs' 2025 Year in Review is dominated by rock/pop artists ([Discogs](https://www.discogs.com/about/news/discogs-year-in-review-2025-is-a-global-snapshot-of-what-music-fans-collected-most/)). Secondary-market note: limited dance pressings "reappear on Discogs at four times the price" ([Divert Records](https://www.divertrecords.sk/story/vinyl-only-culture-is-it-elitist-or-just-misunderstood)).

## B. BEATPORT & DIGITAL RETAIL

**Beatport corporate timeline** ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beatport), [Musically](https://musically.com/2013/02/27/beatport-sold-for-a-reported-50m-to-sfx-entertainment/), [DJ TechTools](https://djtechtools.com/2013/02/26/beatport-sold-to-sfx-entertainment-for-50-million/), [Vice history](https://www.vice.com/en/article/history-beatport-sfx-jonas-tempell-robert-sillerman-lloyd-starr-interview/)):
- Jan 7, 2004: launch with 79 labels; 2005: 100k tracks / 2,700 labels.
- Feb 26, 2013: acquired by SFX for "slightly over $50M" (SEC filings show $58.6M incl. adjustments). Dec 2013: 26 layoffs.
- 2014–15: pivot to streaming/original content; 2015 losses of $5.5M.
- Feb 1, 2016: SFX Chapter 11; Beatport put up for auction; May 10, 2016 auction suspended, streaming/content killed, 49 staff cut, refocus on downloads.
- Dec 2016: SFX emerges as **LiveStyle** (CEO Randy Phillips, ex-AEG; debt cut by $400M; Sillerman out). Beatport "returned to profitability." LiveStyle's stakeholders include Axar Capital (Andrew Axelrod, chairman) and Allianz Global Investors ([Digital DJ Tips, 2018](https://www.digitaldjtips.com/beatport-has-a-new-owner/), [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/pro/sfx-emerges-bankruptcy-new-name-livestyle-new-leader-randy-phillips/), [Fact](https://www.factmag.com/2016/12/07/sfx-new-name-livestyle-shuns-edm/)).
- Oct 2017: Robb McDaniels (INgrooves founder) named CEO ([Grammy.com](https://www.grammy.com/news/ingrooves-founder-robb-mcdaniels-named-new-beatport-ceo/)).
- 2018: buys Pulselocker tech. Feb 2019: Beatsource JV with DJcity. **May 14, 2019: Beatport LINK** streaming into DJ software ($14.99/mo) + Beatport CLOUD ($4.99/mo); 6.6M tracks at launch ([DJ TechTools](https://djtechtools.com/2019/05/14/beatport-link-streaming-for-dj-software-launches-today-beatport-cloud-goes-into-beta/), [MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/beatport-launches-subscription-music-streaming-service-for-djs/)).
- Dec 2020: acquires Loopmasters/Loopcloud/Plugin Boutique ([MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/beatport-buys-loopmasters-acquiring-plugin-boutique-and-loopcloud/)). May 2022: LabelRadar. Feb 2023: majority stake in IMS ([Beatportal](https://www.beatportal.com/articles/147074-beatport-acquires-majority-stake-in-ims-international-music-summit)). 2023: ampsuite ([Beatportal](https://www.beatportal.com/articles/406658-beatport-acquires-ampsuite-and-launches-a-suite-of-professional-products-for-suppliers-artists-and-labels)).
- 2022: sold ~25.5M tracks, ~12% of worldwide digital music sales. Revenue ~$40M (2024) per [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/beatport/__4efPbyITyNEx1wzq85YMlykaAJyJvNbO4ho3a-HTHaU) **[third-party estimate]**.
- **[Unverified]** PitchBook lists a "May 20, 2025 acquisition by LiveStyle"; no press coverage of any 2025 ownership change was found and Beatport appears to have remained under LiveStyle continuously since Dec 2016.

**Pricing:** Beatport tracks now typically $1.29–$2.49, most at $1.49 for MP3, with WAV/AIFF at a surcharge ([House of Tracks](https://houseoftracks.com/faq/what-format-does-beatport-use)); Beatport's own "Why is Beatport increasing prices" support page returned 403, so exact current tiers and effective date are unconfirmed.

**Streaming into DJ software (how DJs acquire music now):** Serato added Tidal/SoundCloud at ADE 2018 (first streaming since Pulselocker's demise); today Serato, rekordbox and djay all integrate Apple Music, Tidal, SoundCloud Go+ ($12.99/mo), Beatport, Beatsource; Spotify returned to rekordbox/Serato/djay in Sept 2025 ([Digital DJ Tips](https://www.digitaldjtips.com/streaming-returns-to-serato-tidal-soundcloud-integrations-announced/), [DJ TechTools](https://djtechtools.com/2025/09/24/spotify-finally-slides-into-rekordbox-serato-and-algoriddim-djay-again-for-better-or-worse/)). Tidal blocked stems use on streamed tracks in Jan 2024 ([DJ TechTools](https://djtechtools.com/2024/01/18/tidal-blocked-djs-from-using-stems-but-the-next-generation-of-dj-gear-will-likely-make-that-irrelevant/)).

**Industry size (IMS Business Report):** 2019 $7.3B → 2020 $3.4B (-54%, COVID; live down 78%) → 2021 $6B → 2023 $12.2B (restated methodology) → 2024 $12.9B/$14.2B (two report bases) → **2025 $15.1B** (+7%), tech house leading Beatport sales ([Music Week 2021](https://www.musicweek.com/live/read/ims-electronic-music-industry-slumps-by-54-during-pandemic/083609), [DJ Mag 2025](https://djmag.com/news/global-electronic-music-industry-value-reaches-129-billion-ims-business-report-2025-finds), [DJ Mag 2026](https://djmag.com/news/global-electronic-music-industry-value-hits-record-151-billion-ims-business-report-reveals), [MusicTech](https://musictech.com/news/industry/ims-electronic-music-business-report-2026/)).

**Symphonic Distribution** ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symphonic_Distribution), [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/person/jorge-brea), [Symphonic blog 2025](https://blog.symphonic.com/2025/12/17/symphonics-year-in-review-2025/)): founded late 2006 in Tampa by Jorge Brea, who had released 45+ vinyl records as a DJ/producer from age 16 (sources confirm the DJ background but none use the "DJ Viro" alias, so that name remains **[Unverified]** outside the thesis). Revenue $500k (2010) → $2.3M (2014) → **$51.5M (2024)** (Tracxn). Funding: $4M Ballast Point (2017); **$37M Series B** led by NewSpring/Ballast Point (Jan 2022). ~200 employees, offices in Brooklyn, Nashville, Bogotá, Mexico, South Africa, Canada, Brazil; 100k+ clients / 500k+ artists; paid out >$100M in 2025 and ~$1B lifetime; 10 Grammy nominations in 2025; Brea won a Music Biz 2024 Bizzy award ([Billboard 2017](https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/symphonic-distribution-digital-music-4-million-funding-8054001)).

**deadmau5 arc:** 7 Grammy nominations, no wins (first 2008 for "The Longest Road" remix; three in 2012; Kx5 album nominated 2024) ([Wikipedia awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_awards_and_nominations_received_by_Deadmau5)); Skrillex/Bieber Twitter feuds 2015 ([Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/skrillex-deadmau5-twitter-feud-6746307/)); Kx5 with Kaskade (2022–23, [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/music/features/kx5-kaskade-deadmau5-album-sxsw-interview-billboard-cover-story-1235282826/)); **sold his catalog and mau5trap (4,000+ songs) to Create Music Group for $55M** (2025) ([RA](https://ra.co/news/82278), [MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/create-music-group-acquires-deadmau5-and-mau5trap-records-catalogs-in-55m-deal/)); Oct 2025 retired the Cube (debuted 2010, final v3 260 tons/600+ LED panels), announced first album in 10 years for 2026 with single "Ameonna", played a 5-hour Red Rocks set Nov 7–8, 2025 including TESTPILOT techno alias; second single "Science" Feb 2, 2026 ([EDM.com](https://edm.com/news/deadmau5-retires-cube-production-reveals-first-album-10-years/), [EDM Identity](https://edmidentity.com/2025/10/24/deadmau5-ameonna-album/)).

## C. THE EDM BOOM AND BUST

**Media/industry turning point 2012:** Skrillex won 3 of 5 Grammy nominations (Feb 2012); Forbes launched "Electronic Cash Kings" (Tiësto #1, $22M) and called EDM "the new rock"; IMS pegged the industry at ~$4B; Sillerman relaunched SFX June 2012 ([Grammy.com](https://www.grammy.com/news/the-year-in-electronica-music/), [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/special-report/2012/0802_top-djs.html), [MBJ](https://www.thembj.org/2012/10/the-rise-of-edm/)). Calvin Harris earned $66M in both 2014 and 2015 (Guinness record) ([Guinness](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/112929-highest-paid-dj-in-one-year)).

**SFX roll-up and collapse (note: the original SFX was sold to Clear Channel for $4.4B on Feb 29, 2000, became Clear Channel Entertainment, spun off as Live Nation in 2005 — the company the thesis author stagehanded for):** [SEC 8-K 2000](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001051253/000095013600000271/0000950136-00-000271.txt/seq-3). SFX 2.0: Disco Donnie Presents was the first acquisition (2012), then ID&T, Made Event (Electric Zoo), React, Flavorus, Totem OneLove, Beatport; **IPO Oct 9, 2013** raised $260M at $13/share, >$1B valuation, Afrojack rang the Nasdaq bell; shares fell to a penny; **Chapter 11 Feb 1, 2016**; emerged Dec 2016 as LiveStyle; Sillerman died Nov 24, 2019, aged 71 ([Music Week](https://www.musicweek.com/live/read/live-music-mogul-robert-fx-sillerman-dies-aged-71/078234), [Billboard obituary](https://www.billboard.com/articles/news/obituary/8545008/robert-sillerman-obituary-sfx-entertainment-final-years/), [Billboard Sillerman interview](https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/edm-sickest-robert-sillerman-sfx-fiasco-feature-7400341/)). LiveStyle was profitable again by 2018 ([Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/articles/business/8490312/livestyle-growth-profits-sfx-bankruptcy-randy-phillips/)).

**TomorrowWorld** (Chattahoochee Hills, GA, 2013–15): 2015 edition stranded thousands overnight in rain; refund fiasco; cancelled March 2016 after SFX bankruptcy ([RA](https://ra.co/news/33693), [Atlanta Magazine](https://www.atlantamagazine.com/news-culture-articles/chaos-in-chattahoochee-hills-rain-catches-tomorrowworld-organizers-ill-prepared-leaving-thousands-stranded-overnight-outside-the-festival/)).

**Insomniac/EDC:** Live Nation bought ~50% of Insomniac for a reported $50M (WSJ, May–June 2013), framed by Rotella as a "creative partnership"; stake appears unchanged since ([Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/live-nation-to-purchase-50-stake-in-insomniac-events-report-1560545/), [Pollstar 30 years](https://news.pollstar.com/2024/01/02/eat-sleep-rave-repeat-pasquale-rotella-celebrates-30-years-of-insomniac/)). EDC moved to Las Vegas June 2011; **EDC LV 2025 (May 16–18): 525,000+ total attendance, 16 stages** ([EDMTunes](https://www.edmtunes.com/2025/05/edc-las-vegas-2025-a-festival-for-the-record-books/)). GA price: $180 (2011) → $470–520 presale (2025) ([Las Vegas Sun 2011](https://lasvegassun.com/news/2011/nov/01/tickets-edc-2012-sale-today/), [festivalviewer](https://festivalviewer.com/edclasvegas/tickets)).

**Ultra Miami:** ~165,000 over 3 days; ~$79M/yr local impact, ~$995M since 2012 by city estimate ([Miami New Times](https://www.miaminewtimes.com/music/ultra-fest-generates-79-million-annually-for-miami-economy-can-edm-save-the-country-6469494/), [EDM.com](https://edm.com/industry/ultra-generated-over-1-billion-for-miami-economy/)). Ultra acquired WMC March 2018 ([Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/ultra-music-festival-winter-music-conference-beijing-8256904/)).

**Electric Zoo:** Aug 30–31, 2013, Jeffrey Russ (23) and Olivia Rotondo (21) died (MDMA/methylone + hyperthermia); Sunday cancelled; dealer Patrick Morgan pleaded guilty Dec 2014 ([DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdny/pr/supplier-molly-resulted-death-electric-zoo-concert-pleads-guilty-manhattan-federal), [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/electric-zoo-canceled-after-two-deaths-during-festival-101287)). Sept 2023 under Avant Gardner: Friday cancelled hours before doors (unpaid 2022 vendors, not "supply chain"), Sunday oversold, fence-jumping; 4+ class actions; 2024 edition cancelled; Avant Gardner/AGDP filed Chapter 11 Aug 2025 after Brooklyn Mirage failed to reopen; settlement with ~60,000 ticket buyers; no 2026 edition ([Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/pro/2023-electric-zoo-festival-class-action-lawsuits-avant-gardner/), [Gothamist](https://gothamist.com/news/avant-gardner-owners-of-brooklyn-mirage-file-for-bankruptcy), [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/bankruptcy-law/electric-zoo-fans-reach-settlement-with-bankrupt-avant-gardner)).

**Bust/plateau 2016–19:** at least seven festivals cancelled for 2016 (two SFX); top-10 DJ earnings grew only 2.5% in 2016 vs 11% prior year; "Dance music has plateaued" ([Billboard 2020 retrospective](https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/dance-music-post-edm-era-analysis-2020-dance-issue-9332803/), [Forbes 2015](https://www.forbes.com/sites/natalierobehmed/2015/10/15/the-future-of-edm/), [DMN](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2016/12/05/edc-nyc-cancelled-edm/)).

**COVID:** WMC and Ultra cancelled March 2020 ([Variety](https://variety.com/2020/music/news/miami-winter-music-conference-canceled-coronavirus-1203528419/)); industry -54% (above); NIVA formed April 2020, Save Our Stages passed Dec 27, 2020 creating the $16.25B Shuttered Venue Operators Grant; NIVA estimated 300+ venues closed permanently in 2020 ([NIVA](https://www.nivassoc.org/sos-act), [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/independent-entertainment-music-venues-save-our-stages-bailout/)).

**Post-COVID festival economics:** 100+ festival cancellations worldwide in 2025; UK AIF: 20 cancelled/postponed/closed by June 10, 2026 and one in five UK indie festivals gone since 2023; 50 Dutch festivals cancelled in 2025; insurance now ~18% of budgets vs 6% in 2019; Bluesfest (Australia, since 1990) liquidated 2026 ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2025/06/01/entertainment/music-festivals-cancellations-pitchfork-cec), [AIF](https://www.aiforg.com/festival-cancellations), [Chartlex tracker](https://www.chartlex.com/blog/touring/music-festival-cancellations-tracker-2026)). Pollstar 2025: top-100 average ticket $132.62 (-2.4% from 2024's $135.92 peak), grosses $8.9B, 60.8% above 2019 ([Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2025/12/23/year-end-business-analysis-a-return-to-earth-2025-grosses-ticket-sales-drop-averages-increase-beyonce-oasis-coldplay-have-top-tours-venues-stadiums-rock/)).

**Live Nation/Ticketmaster antitrust:** DOJ + states sued May 2024; trial began March 2, 2026; DOJ settled mid-trial (Live Nation keeps Ticketmaster, opens venues to rival ticketers, divests some amphitheaters, 15% fee cap, $281M state damages fund); 33 states refused and continued; **April 15, 2026 jury found Live Nation/Ticketmaster liable on every count** (monopolization, illegal tying); remedies, Tunney Act review and appeals pending ([NY AG](https://ag.ny.gov/press-release/2026/attorney-general-james-and-coalition-states-win-trial-against-live-nation-and), [Crowell](https://www.crowell.com/en/insights/client-alerts/after-the-verdict-navigating-the-live-nationticketmaster-antitrust-fallout), [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/music/news/doj-antitrust-attorneys-slam-live-nation-ticketmaster-settlement-1236769787/), [DOJ case page](https://www.justice.gov/atr/case/us-and-plaintiff-states-v-live-nation-entertainment-inc-and-ticketmaster-llc)).

**Underground/rave revival, Gen Z:** Berlin techno culture entered Germany's UNESCO intangible heritage inventory March 13, 2024 after a Rave The Planet campaign begun 2011 (rejected 2022) ([DJ Mag](https://djmag.com/news/berlin-techno-culture-added-unesco-intangible-cultural-heritage-list)); "soft clubbing"/coffee raves up 478% YoY on Eventbrite, 39% of Gen Z don't drink ([Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/blog/press/newsroom/the-new-nightlife-gen-zs-soft-clubbing/)); Charli XCX Boiler Room warehouse rave, Bushwick, Feb 2024 ([Paste](https://www.pastemagazine.com/music/scene-report/the-return-of-electroclash)). **Boiler Room:** bought by DICE 2021, sold Jan 2025 to Superstruct (itself bought by KKR for €1.3B in 2024); ## D. LAW & MEDIA

**RAVE Act aftermath:** Shelley Goldsmith (19) died Aug 31, 2013 of MDMA-related heatstroke at a DC show; mother Dede Goldsmith launched Amend the RAVE Act Aug 2014; 20,000+ signatures; campaign handed to DanceSafe Nov 1, 2018; **DOJ confirmed in 2018 that free water and fact-based drug education are "reasonable and appropriate safety measures" not violating the Act**; no prosecutions have ever been brought under the RAVE Act, but promoters' lawyers still bar harm-reduction services ([Amend the RAVE Act](https://amendtheraveact.org/whats-new/), [DanceSafe](https://dancesafe.org/amend-the-rave-act/), [Your EDM](https://www.youredm.com/2018/10/09/amend-rave-act-campaign/), [Filter](https://filtermag.org/festival-drug-checking/), [Lucid News on Biden](https://www.lucid.news/biden-rave-act-impede-risk-reduction/)). Biden never amended his own law; DPA/DanceSafe petitioned him as VP ([DPA](https://engage.drugpolicy.org/secure/vp-biden-fix-your-rave-act-and-save-lives)).

**Utah 2005 (Versus II, Spanish Fork Canyon, Aug 20, 2005, Sheriff James Tracy):** federal suit dismissed *with prejudice* after plaintiffs sought a voluntary dismissal to build a class action ([KSL](https://www.ksl.com/article/331875/judge-dismisses-rave-bust-lawsuit), [Deseret News](https://www.deseret.com/2005/10/9/19916524/did-utah-county-sheriff-plan-rave-raid/), [ACLU order](https://www.aclu.org/sites/default/files/FilesPDFs/utah_order%20denying%20tro%20motion.pdf)).

**Drug checking law:** 45 states + DC now permit fentanyl test strips (holdouts: ID, IN, IA, ND, TX); 18 states changed drug-checking laws Sept 2023–Aug 2024; WI and WV legalized xylazine strips 2024; federal bills (Welch/Booker "Expanding Nationwide Access to Test Strips Act" S.2484, July 2023; Coons/Cornyn "Fentanyl Safe Testing and Overdose Prevention Act") introduced but no evidence of passage; New York runs state drug-checking pilots and A808 (2025) would create a formal program; NSW Australia running festival drug-checking trials at Lost Paradise, EPIK, Field Day (Dec 2025) ([Network for PHL 50-state](https://www.networkforphl.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/2024-50-State-DCE-Fact-Sheet.pdf), [Congress.gov S.2484](https://www.congress.gov/bill/118th-congress/senate-bill/2484/text), [NY DOH](https://www.health.ny.gov/diseases/aids/consumers/prevention/oduh/drug_checking.htm), [NSW Health](https://www.health.nsw.gov.au/news/Pages/20251229_00.aspx)).

**UK:** CJPOA 1994 s.63 ("repetitive beats") still on the books; 30th anniversary coverage in 2024 drew parallels to new protest laws ([DJ Mag](https://djmag.com/features/dance-protest-30-years-uks-anti-rave-criminal-justice-bill), [RA](https://ra.co/news/77299), [legislation.gov.uk](https://legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/33/section/63/enacted)); £10,000 fines for unlicensed music events introduced Aug 28, 2020 (11 issued the first weekend, incl. a 3,000-person Banwen rave; Met broke up 21 UMEs in one night) ([IQ](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2020/08/first-10k-penalties-issued-illegal-parties/)). **NTIA:** UK nightclubs down 32.7% since March 2020 (1,240 → 835 by Nov 2024; 405 closures; 348 of them independents); 26% of all late-night venues lost 2020–25; three net closures/week mid-2025, blamed on NICs/minimum wage; NTIA projects "last night out 31.12.2029" at current rates ([NTIA](https://ntia.co.uk/ntia-figures-reveal-extent-of-crisis-uk-nightclubs-struggle-as-dance-floors-nationwide-shut-down/), [NTIA Aug 2025](https://ntia.co.uk/closures-in-britains-late-night-venues-reach-all-time-high-with-one-in-four-businesses-lost-since-2020/), [Spirits Business](https://www.thespiritsbusiness.com/2025/01/uk-nightclub-sector-loses-405-venues/)).

**Berlin "Clubsterben":** Watergate closed NYE 2024 after 22 years (rents, inflation, "shift in relevance of club culture"); SchwuZ bankruptcy 2024; Wilde Renate lease/fire troubles 2025; ARD documentary "Clubsterben" ([The Berliner](https://www.the-berliner.com/politics/berlin-club-death-klubsterben-watergate-renate-inflation-inflation/), [DJ Mag](https://djmag.com/news/berlin-club-closures-investigated-new-documentary-watch), [Euronews](https://euronews.com/culture/2024/09/18/famous-berlin-nightclub-watergate-to-close-its-doors-after-22-years)).

**US cities:** NYC repealed the 1926 Cabaret Law Nov 27, 2017 and created an Office of Nightlife (zoning restrictions on dancing survived); 311 noise complaints 610k (2024, +19%) and 636k (2025); Brooklyn council members targeting Navy Yard warehouse raves ([NYC Mayor](https://www.nyc.gov/office-of-the-mayor/news/742-17/mayor-de-blasio-signs-legislation-repeal-cabaret-law), [Gothamist](https://gothamist.com/news/brooklyn-city-councilmembers-look-to-silence-navy-yards-noisy-raves), [Gothamist zoning](https://gothamist.com/arts-entertainment/nyc-cabaret-law-legislation)).
