# Agent report: the venue

*Unedited output of the venue-research pass. Mirrors `research/agent-reports/02-shawnee-cave-venue-timeline.md`.*


Research agent output, September 16, 2026. ~60 fetches plus Wayback Machine pulls (made before the Internet Archive went offline mid-session). Web-search budget exhausted at 200.

**Location:** 3747 Illinois Route 127 ("Black Diamond Hwy"), ~6–7 mi south of Murphysboro, near Pomona / across from Cedar Lake, Jackson County, IL. ~50–53 acres; ~125–215 ft bluff shelter; "Illinois' largest natural rock shelter." Names used over time: Salt Petre Cave Park, Saltpetre/Saltpeter Cave, Shawnee Salt Petre Cave Natural Amphitheatre, Shawnee Saltpetre Cave Theater, Shawnee Natural Amphitheatre, "The Cave," and (2020–) Shawnee Cave Amphitheater. ([showcaves.com](https://www.showcaves.com/english/usa/showcaves/Shawnee.html), [shawneecave.com 2001 archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20010722194117/http://shawneecave.com/))

Legend: **[C]** = confirmed by a cited source; **[I]** = inference; **[NF]** = searched, not found.

---

## 1. Origins (1960s–1990s)

- **[C] Late 1960s / 1969** — Gib Todd of Murphysboro built the venue and made it a "nationally known" stop. Acts cited: Head East, Tanya Tucker (age 15; a 1976 news clip exists), Dr. Hook & the Medicine Show, Muddy Waters, T.G. Sheppard. ([KFVS, Oct 16 2020](https://www.kfvs12.com/2020/10/16/live-music-return-historic-cave-amphitheater/); [Bluegrass Today, Apr 5 2021](https://bluegrasstoday.com/shawnee-cave-amphitheater-to-reopen-for-memorial-day-event/); [Daily Yonder Q&A with Shane Wade, May 26 2023](https://dailyyonder.com/qa-organizing-a-music-festival-underground/2023/05/26/))
- **[C] 1974** — Per the Daily Egyptian, "Saltpetre Cave concerts began in 1974 with four or five concerts a year," including Ozark Mountain Daredevils and Steve Miller Band; they "continued until the late 1980s, when the owner was no longer able to carry the tradition on." ([Daily Egyptian, June 23 2000](https://dailyegyptian.com/37898/archives/saltpetre-cavebringing-back-the-70s/)) (Note the 1969 vs 1974 discrepancy between sources.)
- **[C] Sept 17, 1978** — Muddy Waters setlist logged at the venue. ([setlist.fm](https://www.setlist.fm/venue/shawnee-cave-amphitheater-murphysboro-il-usa-bd3c51e.html?year=1978))
- **[C] 1978** — Bob Goodale (SIUC student 1976–77) helped organize the "Shawnee Jamboree" concerts there. (Daily Egyptian 2000, above)
- **[C] Late 1980s–1999** — dormant as a concert venue. A 2001 visitor recalls paddle boats and an unstaffed open gate; Shane Wade recalls it being open to the public for paddle-boating when he was in junior high. ([mycorneronline](https://www.mycorneronline.com/photography/photo13_242.html); Daily Yonder)

## 2. Goodale era begins (2000–2002)

- **[C] ~2000** — Bob and Lou Goodale become "new owners." Goodale: "We're bringing the cave back to the 70s." First rock show since the '80s: June 2000 with No Quarter (Zeppelin tribute), Uncle Pacos & the Country Funkins, Hurd Brothers, Stank Willie & the Red Hots; $10/$15, $3 parking. (Daily Egyptian, June 23 2000)
- **[C]** A 2008 JamBase feature says Goodale "somehow saved the life of the previous owner of the cave and was able to purchase the property at a very reasonable price"; his prior businesses were ice cream, coal and firewood; roots in Centralia; nicknamed "Caveman Bob" / "Santa." ([JamBase, Sept 17 2008](https://www.jambase.com/article/strip-mines-music-festival-08-29-31-il))
- **[C] 2001** — shawneecave.com live (email shawneecaveman@aol.com, "Non profit organization," capacity 4,000, 618-687-9663). Listed: April 28–29, 2001 David Nelson Band / Brooklyn Cowboys w/ Buddy Cage / St. Stephen's Blues Band; Reggae show Sept 8, 2001 ($12/$15, camping $10/tent). Domain expired Jan 24, 2005. ([Wayback 2001 home](https://web.archive.org/web/20010722194117/http://shawneecave.com/), [events.htm](https://web.archive.org/web/20010803004046/http://shawneecave.com/events.htm); [Daily Egyptian, Apr 27 2001](https://dailyegyptian.com/35530/archives/classic-rockers-to-play-shawnee-cave/))
- **[C] July 4, 2002** — Nazareth, w/ Drivin' Rain, Sinical, Joyride; $18; promoter Jeff Armintrout. ([Daily Egyptian, June 28 2002](https://dailyegyptian.com/32773/archives/nazareth-to-rock-out-shawnee-cave-on-fourth/))

## 5. Decline (2009–2012)

- **[C] Apr 24, 2009** — 420 Show/Cave Fest: Yo Mama's Big Fat Booty Band, Zoogma, Kuang, Dervish, Andy B; a Rollitup thread still calls it "one of the largest electronic music festivals in the US." (Songkick; [Rollitup](https://www.rollitup.org/t/cavefest-in-carbondale-il.168943/))
- **[C] Apr 23, 2010** — 420 Show: Kilowatts. **Oct 1–2, 2010** — Harvest Fest 2010: Soul Mission, Fer De Lance, Pink Floyd tribute, "Crash and Burn Techno & Electronic Stage"; $25/$30. ([cavefest.fws1.com, Oct 2010](https://web.archive.org/web/20101013064728/http://www.cavefest.fws1.com/))
- **[C] Aug 2010** — Sierra Club visitor note: $5/person admission, owner's main business is firewood + festivals, boating discontinued "due to insurance concerns." ([Shawnee Group blog](https://shawneegroup.blogspot.com/2010/08/shawnee-saltpeter-cave.html))
- **[C] Apr 22–23, 2011** — The Original 420 Show 2011: Henhouse Prowlers, Jaik Willis, Messy Jiverson, Swamp Foot, Another Dead Cover Band + a dubstep second stage run by Digital-Glitch; $30/$40. **July 8–9, 2011** — Caveblast 2011 (last event on Goodale's site). ([cavefest.fws1.com 2011–2013 captures](https://web.archive.org/web/20130601000000/http://www.cavefest.fws1.com/))
- **[C] Aug 31, 2012** — Sierra Club postscript: gate closed with a "for sale" sign. (blog above)
- **[C] Sept 14, 2012** — setlist.fm logs a House of Heroes show at the venue; unverified context. ([setlist.fm](https://www.setlist.fm/venue/shawnee-cave-amphitheater-murphysboro-il-usa-bd3c51e.html?year=2012))
- **[C] Shane Wade's account (2023):** "in the 2000s it became an underground EDM scene, which... got out of hand with some of the partying... By probably 2008 or 2009 it shut down. My partner acquired it in 2012 or 2013 and then we just let it sit for a while, let the smoke clear... The city leaders had a bad taste in their mouths from that." (Daily Yonder) Showcaves.com separately notes "violence and drug use" prompted "regular discussions among police and local politicians."
- **[C]** The 2020 Southern Illinoisan reopening story calls Goodale "the last face of the cave" before closure. ([The Southern, Oct 9 2020](https://thesouthern.com/news/local/shawnee-cave-amphitheater-to-reopen-at-the-end-of-the-month/article_97b8f197-423a-5afc-a497-0dcdc894c529.html))
- **Contradiction:** Bluegrass Today says the venue "fell into foreclosure before Jeff Parrish purchased it in 2004." Given Goodale ran events through July 2011 and Wade himself says 2012/2013, "2004" is almost certainly an error. **[I]**
